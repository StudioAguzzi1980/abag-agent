# 18 — Lead Research & Qualification

## Purpose

This module provides ABAG consultants and business development professionals with a structured framework for researching, qualifying, and evaluating prospective U.S. clients who may be interested in expanding into Italy or Europe.

The materials in this section are designed to be used sequentially — from initial desk research before the first conversation, through the discovery call, and finally to a scored recommendation on whether to pursue the opportunity.

## How to Use This Module

1. **Before the call:** Run through `01_Company_Analysis.md` to build your intelligence file. Use `02_Expansion_Readiness.md` and `03_Growth_Indicators.md` to form an initial hypothesis.
2. **Pre-call check:** Complete `04_Lead_Qualification_Checklist.md` to confirm the lead is worth pursuing.
3. **During the call:** Use `05_Discovery_Call_Checklist.md` as your live guide and ask the questions from `06_First_Meeting_Questions.md`.
4. **After the call:** Complete the scoring worksheet in `07_Scoring_System.md` to quantify the opportunity.
5. **Quality control:** Review `08_Common_Mistakes.md` and `09_Ideal_vs_NonIdeal_Prospects.md` to calibrate your judgment.
