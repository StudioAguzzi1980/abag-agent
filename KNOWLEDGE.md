## Services

### 1. Company Formation in Italy

ABAG guides clients through every step of incorporating a legal entity in Italy. This includes selecting the appropriate corporate structure, preparing and filing the required documentation, and coordinating with Italian notaries, the Chamber of Commerce, and tax authorities.

**Common entity types:**
- **S.r.l. (Società a Responsabilità Limitata)** — The Italian equivalent of a limited liability company (LLC). The most common structure for small to mid-size foreign-owned businesses. Requires a minimum share capital of €1 (simplified SRL) or €10,000 (standard SRL).
- **S.p.A. (Società per Azioni)** — A joint-stock company suited for larger enterprises. Requires a minimum share capital of €50,000.
- **Branch Office (Sede Secondaria)** — An extension of a foreign parent company operating in Italy. Not a separate legal entity; the parent company remains fully liable.
- **Representative Office (Ufficio di Rappresentanza)** — A non-commercial presence used for market research, promotion, and liaison activities. Cannot generate revenue directly.

**Key steps in the incorporation process:**
- Obtain Italian tax codes (codice fiscale) for all directors and shareholders
- Draft and notarize the articles of incorporation (atto costitutivo) and company bylaws (statuto)
- Register with the Italian Business Register (Registro delle Imprese)
- Register with the Italian Revenue Agency (Agenzia delle Entrate) for VAT (Partita IVA)
- Open a corporate bank account
- Register for INPS (social security) if the company will employ staff

### 3. Tax Advisory

ABAG provides expert guidance on Italian and cross-border tax matters for foreign-owned businesses and expatriates. Italian taxation can be complex, particularly for U.S. entities subject to both Italian and American tax obligations.

**Areas covered:**
- Italian corporate income tax (IRES — Imposta sul Reddito delle Società, currently 24%)
- Regional production tax (IRAP — Imposta Regionale sulle Attività Produttive, typically 3.9%)
- Value Added Tax (IVA/VAT) registration, compliance, and reporting
- U.S.–Italy double taxation treaty (Convention for the Avoidance of Double Taxation, signed 1984, revised 1999)
- Transfer pricing compliance for intragroup transactions
- Withholding taxes on dividends, interest, and royalties
- Tax planning for corporate structures and profit repatriation
- Personal income tax (IRPEF) for executives and employees relocated to Italy
- Italian flat-tax regime for new residents (Regime dei Neo-Residenti) — potentially relevant for high-net-worth individuals

### 5. Corporate Compliance

Operating a company in Italy involves ongoing regulatory and governance obligations. ABAG helps clients stay compliant and avoid penalties.

**Compliance areas:**
- Annual filings with the Italian Business Register (Registro delle Imprese)
- Corporate governance: shareholders' meetings, board resolutions, minutes
- Statutory reporting obligations for S.r.l. and S.p.A. entities
- Anti-money laundering (AML) compliance under Italian and EU law
- Data protection and GDPR compliance for businesses processing personal data
- Labor law compliance: employment contracts, collective agreements (CCNL), mandatory notifications
- Industry-specific licenses and authorizations where applicable
- Registered office and legal representative maintenance

### 6. Banking Support

One of the most practical challenges for foreign businesses entering Italy is opening a corporate bank account. ABAG provides dedicated support to navigate the Italian banking system.

**Services include:**
- Identification of suitable banking partners based on the client's business profile
- Preparation of the documentation package required by Italian banks (KYC, AML, corporate documents)
- Facilitation of meetings and introductions with banking representatives
- Support with online and remote account opening procedures where available
- Guidance on multi-currency accounts and international wire transfer capabilities
- Introductions to payment service providers and fintech solutions as alternatives or supplements to traditional banking

**Note:** Italian banks typically require a physical or virtual registered office address, a valid Italian tax code (codice fiscale), and a completed company registration before a corporate account can be opened.

### 7. Lead Research

ABAG provides targeted lead research services to help American companies identify and evaluate potential business partners, clients, distributors, and commercial opportunities in the Italian and European markets. This service supports both pre-entry due diligence and active market development.

**Typical use cases:**
- Identifying distributors, agents, or resellers for a product or service in Italy
- Sourcing potential B2B customers or end-users in a specific Italian sector or region
- Researching Italian companies for partnership, joint venture, or acquisition purposes
- Mapping the competitive landscape and identifying key players in a target market
- Qualifying inbound interest from Italian companies seeking American partners

**Research methodology:**
- Database and registry research using official Italian and EU business sources (e.g., Camera di Commercio databases, Registro delle Imprese, Cerved)
- Sector-specific industry directories and trade association memberships
- Trade show and event intelligence for relevant Italian and European industries
- Direct outreach and qualification of identified prospects where required
- Cross-referencing with ABAG's existing professional network in Italy

**Deliverables typically include:**
- A curated long list of potential leads with company name, sector, size, and contact information
- A qualified short list with enriched profiles: key contacts, business activities, recent news, and fit assessment
- Recommendations on how to approach identified leads given the client's market entry strategy
- Optional ongoing lead monitoring and refreshing as the client's pipeline evolves

**Key Italian and EU data sources used:**
- Registro delle Imprese (Business Register) for company registration and financial data
- Cerved and CRIF for credit information and company intelligence
- ICE Agenzia (Italian Trade and Investment Agency) for sector and export data
- Confindustria and sector-specific industry associations
- EU open data portals and Eurostat for macro-level market intelligence

### 8. Business Expansion from the U.S. to Italy and Europe

ABAG's flagship offering is comprehensive end-to-end support for American companies and entrepreneurs making the move into Italy and the European market. This service integrates all of the above disciplines into a coordinated expansion program.

**Typical engagement scope:**
- Initial feasibility assessment: evaluating the viability of the Italian market for the client's industry and business model
- Legal structure selection and company formation
- Tax structuring and U.S.–Italy tax planning
- Banking setup and initial financial infrastructure
- HR and employment setup if staff will be hired locally
- Regulatory and licensing review for the specific sector
- Ongoing advisory retainer for post-launch support
- Connections to ABAG's professional network: legal counsel, real estate, logistics, and sector specialists

**Why Italy as an entry point to Europe:**
- Italy is the third-largest economy in the Eurozone
- Strategic geographic position as a gateway to Southern Europe and the Mediterranean
- Strong industrial sectors: manufacturing, fashion, food & beverage, technology, tourism, and luxury goods
- EU single market access from a single Italian base
- Favorable incentive programs for foreign investment and startup activity
