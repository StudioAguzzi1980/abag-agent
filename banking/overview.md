# Italian Banking — Overview

## Introduction

The Italian banking system is one of the largest in the Eurozone. For U.S. companies establishing an Italian presence, the banking relationship is foundational — it enables the company to receive revenue, pay employees and suppliers, and meet Italian tax obligations.

However, the Italian banking system presents unique challenges for foreign-owned businesses, including strict KYC and AML requirements, language barriers, and bureaucratic onboarding processes. ABAG specializes in navigating these challenges.

## Regulatory Framework

### Key Regulatory Bodies

| Body | Function |
|---|---|
| **Banca d'Italia** | Central bank; banking sector regulator |
| **IVASS** | Insurance sector regulator |
| **Consob** | Securities market regulator |
| **European Central Bank (ECB)** | Prudential regulator for significant Italian banks |
| **EBA (European Banking Authority)** | EU-level banking rules and standards |

### AML and KYC Requirements

Italian banks are subject to D.Lgs. 231/2007 (implementing EU AML Directives). Enhanced due diligence is required for:
- Non-EU beneficial owners
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
- Complex ownership structures
- High-risk jurisdictions (though the U.S. is not high-risk)
- High-value or unusual transaction patterns

## Payment Infrastructure

### SEPA Payments

Italy is part of the **Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA)**, which enables fast, low-cost EUR transfers within the Eurozone:
- **SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT):** EUR transfers to any SEPA country (42 countries) — typically same-day within Italy
- **SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst):** Real-time transfers (24/7)
- **SEPA Direct Debit (SDD):** Automated collections from Italian customers

### International (SWIFT) Transfers

For USD transactions and transfers to/from the U.S.:
- Processed via SWIFT network
- Typically 1–3 business days
- Correspondent banking fees apply
- FATCA compliance documentation may be required

### Italian Payment Methods

Italian businesses commonly use:
- **Bonifico** (bank transfer) — the standard B2B payment method
- **RID / SDD** (direct debit) — for recurring payments
- **RI.BA** (receipt-backed payment) — common in Italian business
- **MAV and RAV** — payment via payment notice at bank
- **Credit/debit cards** — primarily for retail (Visa, Mastercard, Bancomat)
