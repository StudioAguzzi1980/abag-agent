# Banking Documentation Requirements

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive checklist of documentation required to open an Italian corporate bank account. Requirements vary by bank, but this covers all items commonly requested by Italian banking institutions.

Prepare all documents **before** the bank meeting to avoid delays.

---

## SECTION A — Company Documents

### A1 — Italian Company Documents

- [ ] **Visura Camerale** (Business Register extract)
  - Issued by the Camera di Commercio
  - Must be dated within **3 months** of submission
  - ABAG obtains this on the client's behalf

- [ ] **Atto Costitutivo** (Articles of Incorporation)
  - Notarial deed executed at incorporation
  - Official copy from the Registro delle Imprese

- [ ] **Statuto** (Company Bylaws)
  - Current version; must match the Business Register

- [ ] **Certificate of VAT registration (Partita IVA)**
  - Confirmation of VAT number issued by Agenzia delle Entrate

- [ ] **Board Resolution / Shareholder Resolution**
  - Authorizing the opening of the bank account
  - Designating the authorized signatories and their signing limits

- [ ] **Company description letter** (ABAG prepares)
  - In Italian and English
  - Describes: business activity, products/services, Italian clients and suppliers, expected transaction volumes, purpose of account

---

## SECTION B — Individual Documents (All Beneficial Owners, Directors, Authorized Signatories)

Required for every individual who is:
- A shareholder or beneficial owner with ≥25% ownership (or effective control)
- A director or legal representative (legale rappresentante)
- An authorized signatory on the account

### B1 — Identity Verification

- [ ] **Valid passport** — color copy of photo page; must be valid with ≥12 months remaining
- [ ] **Italian tax code (codice fiscale)** — required for all Italian residents and Italian company officers

### B2 — Address Verification

- [ ] **Proof of residential address** — utility bill or bank statement, no older than 3 months
  - Must match the address declared to the bank
  - For U.S. addresses: a U.S. bank statement or utility bill in English is generally accepted with Italian translation

### B3 — Source of Funds and Wealth (if requested)

- [ ] **Source of wealth declaration** — if requested by the bank; explains the origin of the capital invested in the Italian company
- [ ] Supporting documentation (tax return extract, investment account statement, etc.) may be requested

---

## SECTION C — U.S. Parent / Corporate Shareholder Documents

Required when the shareholder is a U.S. company or other legal entity.

- [ ] **Certificate of Incorporation** — with apostille + certified Italian translation
- [ ] **Articles of Incorporation / Operating Agreement** — with apostille + certified Italian translation
- [ ] **Certificate of Good Standing** — issued within 6 months; with apostille + certified Italian translation
- [ ] **Incumbency Certificate or Officer Certificate** — confirming current authorized signatories; with apostille + certified Italian translation
- [ ] **EIN (Employer Identification Number) confirmation** — U.S. tax ID of the parent entity
- [ ] **FATCA documentation** — W-8 or W-9 form (banks subject to FATCA will request this for U.S.-connected entities)

---

## SECTION D — Business Activity Supporting Documents

Some banks (particularly for new companies) also request:

- [ ] **Business plan** (1–3 pages) — expected revenues, expenses, and commercial rationale for Italy
- [ ] **Letter of introduction from U.S. correspondent bank** — if available; confirms the company's existing banking relationship
- [ ] **Contracts or letters of intent** — with expected Italian clients or suppliers, demonstrating expected transaction flows
- [ ] **Website URL** — banks may review the company's online presence as part of due diligence

---

## Document Preparation Notes

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Language | Most documents must be provided in Italian or with certified Italian translation |
| Apostille | Required for U.S. official documents (Secretary of State apostille) |
| Translation | Certified Italian translation by a sworn translator |
| Date validity | Certificates of Good Standing, Visura Camerale, and proof of address must be current |
| Originals vs. copies | Some banks require original documents; others accept certified copies — confirm with ABAG |
