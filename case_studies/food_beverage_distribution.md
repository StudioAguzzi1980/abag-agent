# Case Study: U.S. Food & Beverage Brand — Italian Market Entry

## Client Profile

| | |
|---|---|
| **Industry** | Consumer packaged goods (CPG) / Food & Beverage |
| **Headquarters** | Austin, TX |
| **Product** | Premium functional beverages (wellness-positioned) |
| **Italy objective** | Distribute product in Italian specialty retail and HORECA channels; test viability of European expansion via Italy |
| **Timeline** | First Italian sales within 6 months |

---

## Challenge

The client had built a successful DTC brand in the U.S. and received several inbound inquiries from Italian importers who had discovered the brand at international trade events. The company's founder wanted to test the Italian market but was uncertain whether to:
1. Work through a third-party Italian distributor (lighter-touch entry)
2. Establish an Italian subsidiary with direct market presence
3. Begin with online sales to Italian consumers to test demand

Additional challenges:
- The product contained ingredients that required EU novel food and labeling assessment
- The company had no Italian-language marketing materials
- Prior outreach to a Milan trade law firm had resulted in a quote for a complex S.r.l. setup the founder believed was unnecessary given the exploratory nature of the project
- No Italian banking relationship; required ability to receive EUR payments quickly

---

## ABAG Solution

### Phase 1 — Market Entry Strategy (Weeks 1–4)

ABAG conducted a focused market entry assessment covering:
- Italian functional beverage market size and growth trends
- Regulatory pathway for EU/Italian market entry (labeling, novel food, health claims)
- Competitive landscape mapping (key domestic and international brands in the wellness beverage category)
- Channel analysis: specialty health retailers, organic food stores, gyms and HORECA, e-commerce
- Distributor landscape: identification of 5 candidate Italian specialty distributors

**Recommendation:** Distributor-first entry to minimize upfront investment; establish S.r.l. only once distribution economics are validated. First year: operate under the U.S. entity exporting to an Italian distributor.

### Phase 2 — Regulatory Pre-Assessment (Weeks 2–5, parallel)

ABAG coordinated with an Italian food regulatory consultant to:
- Review the product's ingredient list for EU compliance
- Identify Italian-language labeling requirements (mandatory elements, format, font size)
- Flag any health claim restrictions under EU Regulation 1924/2006
- Advise on import documentation for U.S.-origin goods entering Italy/EU

**Finding:** One ingredient required review under EU Regulation 2015/2283 (Novel Food); timeline for regulatory clearance estimated at 4–6 months.

### Phase 3 — Distributor Identification (Weeks 4–8)

ABAG facilitated introductions to 3 Italian specialty food/beverage distributors. Key criteria:
- Coverage of specialty health retailers (Naturasì, Bio c' Bon, independent health stores) and select HORECA
- Experience with premium imported U.S. brands
- Willingness to work with a small initial volume commitment

The client signed a 2-year distribution agreement with a Milanese distributor, covering Northern Italy initially.

### Phase 4 — Basic Italian Infrastructure (Weeks 6–10)

While full S.r.l. formation was deferred, ABAG helped the client establish:
- A **Wise Business account** to receive EUR payments from the Italian distributor before the S.r.l. was established
- An **Italian-language product website landing page** with GDPR-compliant cookie banner
- Basic invoicing documentation compliant with Italian import/export requirements

## Key Lessons

- **Not every Italy entry requires an S.r.l. from day one** — a distributor-first approach can validate the market at much lower cost
- **Regulatory compliance must be addressed before market entry**, not after — the novel food issue would have delayed the entry by 6+ months if discovered after the distributor agreement was signed
- **Italian labeling must be perfect before the first shipment** — Italian customs and the Ministry of Health inspect labels on imported food products
- **ABAG's distributor network** saved 3–4 months of market scanning the client would otherwise have done independently
