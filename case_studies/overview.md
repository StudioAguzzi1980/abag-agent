# Case Studies — Overview

This section contains anonymized or representative case studies of ABAG client engagements. These case studies illustrate the range of challenges U.S. companies face when entering Italy and the role ABAG plays in resolving them.

All client names and identifying details have been changed or omitted to protect confidentiality.
