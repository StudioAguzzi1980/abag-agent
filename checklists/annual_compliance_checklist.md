# Checklist: Annual Compliance Obligations — Italian S.r.l.

> **Instructions:** This checklist covers the standard annual compliance obligations for an Italian S.r.l. with a December 31 fiscal year-end. Update dates if your company has a different fiscal year. Review with your ABAG project manager at the beginning of each year.

---

## JANUARY

- [ ] Review compliance calendar with ABAG for the new year
- [ ] Confirm accounting records are complete for the prior year
- [ ] Begin preparation of annual VAT declaration (dichiarazione IVA) for prior year
- [ ] Confirm INPS/INAIL contribution reconciliation for prior year

---

## FEBRUARY

- [ ] **[DEADLINE: Feb 28]** File annual VAT declaration (dichiarazione IVA) for prior year
- [ ] Review any open tax positions from prior year
- [ ] Board meeting or written resolution to schedule annual shareholders' meeting

---

## MARCH

- [ ] **[DEADLINE: Mar 31]** Issue CU (Certificazione Unica) forms to all employees and contractors
- [ ] Draft financial statements (bilancio) for prior year — ABAG begins preparation
- [ ] Prepare and approve directors' report (relazione sulla gestione) if required

---

## APRIL

- [ ] Annual shareholders' meeting convened — if holding within 120 days of Dec 31 year-end
- [ ] **[DEADLINE: April 30]** Annual shareholders' meeting to approve accounts (120-day rule; unless 180-day window applies)
- [ ] Q1 VAT return filed (if quarterly filer)
- [ ] File any outstanding Intrastat declarations for Q4 of prior year

---

## MAY

- [ ] Prepare and file annual accounts with Chamber of Commerce (within 30 days of shareholder approval)
- [ ] Annual compliance review meeting with ABAG
- [ ] Confirm directors' appointments are current in Business Register

---

## JUNE

- [ ] **[DEADLINE: Jun 30]** File annual accounts with Chamber of Commerce (if approved in May)
- [ ] **[DEADLINE: Jun 30 – exact date varies]** First IRES/IRAP advance payment (acconto)
- [ ] Annual shareholders' meeting — if using 180-day window (companies with specific governance requirements)
- [ ] Review beneficial ownership (Titolare Effettivo) declaration — update if ownership changed
- [ ] Q2 VAT return filed (if quarterly filer)

---

## JULY

- [ ] Mid-year financial review with ABAG management accounts
- [ ] Confirm any corporate changes since January (directors, address, capital) are filed with Business Register

---

## AUGUST

- [ ] Limited activities (Italian summer holiday period — Italian authorities operate at reduced capacity)
- [ ] No major compliance deadlines — use to prepare documents for Q3

---

## SEPTEMBER

- [ ] Q3 VAT return preparation begins
- [ ] Review payroll data for 770 filing preparation
- [ ] Check open items from mid-year financial review

---

## OCTOBER

- [ ] **[DEADLINE: Oct 31]** File annual corporate income tax return (Modello Redditi SC)
- [ ] Q3 VAT return filed (if quarterly filer)
- [ ] 770 return (annual withholding tax return) filed — confirm deadline with ABAG for current year

---

## NOVEMBER

- [ ] **[DEADLINE: Nov 30]** Second IRES/IRAP advance payment (acconto)
- [ ] Year-end tax planning review with ABAG — identify any actions before December 31
- [ ] Review and update GDPR documentation if needed

---

## DECEMBER

- [ ] Accounting close planning — confirm all transactions are recorded
- [ ] Inventory count (if applicable)
- [ ] Year-end accruals preparation
- [ ] Review of AML risk assessment and beneficial ownership records
- [ ] Update any statutory books or Business Register filings before year-end
- [ ] Plan Q4 VAT return (if quarterly filer — due in February next year)
- [ ] Confirm INPS and INAIL position for the year
- [ ] Brief board/directors on key compliance items for the new year

---

## MONTHLY / ONGOING (Year-Round)

- [ ] Monthly VAT return and payment (if monthly filer) — due by 16th of following month
- [ ] Payroll processing and F24 payment (if employees) — monthly
- [ ] Electronic invoicing compliance (SDI) — all invoices via SDI
- [ ] INPS contributions (employees) — monthly F24 payment
- [ ] Update Business Register within 30 days of any corporate change
