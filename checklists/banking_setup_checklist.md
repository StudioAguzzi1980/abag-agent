# Checklist: Italian Corporate Bank Account Opening

> **Instructions:** Complete all items before the bank meeting. Items marked [ABAG] will be prepared by ABAG. Items marked [CLIENT] require input or action from the client. Items marked [BOTH] require joint effort.

---

## Pre-Banking Prerequisites

- [ ] [CLIENT/ABAG] Italian company fully registered (Visura Camerale available)
- [ ] [CLIENT/ABAG] Italian tax code (codice fiscale) issued for company
- [ ] [CLIENT/ABAG] VAT number (Partita IVA) issued
- [ ] [CLIENT/ABAG] Italian registered address confirmed

---

## SECTION A — Company Documents

- [ ] [ABAG] Visura Camerale — current (within 3 months)
- [ ] [ABAG] Atto Costitutivo (certified copy)
- [ ] [ABAG] Statuto (current version)
- [ ] [ABAG] Partita IVA certificate
- [ ] [ABAG] Board/shareholder resolution authorizing account opening and designating authorized signatories
- [ ] [ABAG] Company description letter in Italian (describing business activity, expected transactions)
- [ ] [ABAG] Organizational chart showing ownership structure

---

## SECTION B — Individual Documents (All Beneficial Owners, Directors, Signatories)

For each individual:

**Individual 1: ______________________**
- [ ] [CLIENT] Valid passport — color copy
- [ ] [CLIENT] Proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement, <3 months)
- [ ] [CLIENT/ABAG] Codice fiscale
- [ ] [CLIENT] Source of funds declaration (if requested by bank)

**Individual 2: ______________________** (copy above for each additional person)

---

## SECTION C — U.S. Corporate Shareholder Documents

- [ ] [CLIENT] Certificate of Incorporation — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] [CLIENT] Articles/Operating Agreement — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] [CLIENT] Certificate of Good Standing — apostilled + certified Italian translation (within 6 months)
- [ ] [CLIENT] Incumbency Certificate — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] [CLIENT] EIN confirmation letter (from IRS or equivalent)
- [ ] [ABAG] FATCA documentation (W-8BEN-E or equivalent) — prepared in coordination with client

---

## SECTION D — Business Support Documents (if required by selected bank)

- [ ] [ABAG/CLIENT] Business plan (1–3 pages) — expected Italian revenues and expenses
- [ ] [CLIENT] Reference letter from U.S. correspondent bank (if available)
- [ ] [CLIENT] Sample contracts or LOIs with Italian customers/suppliers (if available)
- [ ] [CLIENT] U.S. parent company's most recent audited financial statements or tax return

---

## SECTION E — Bank Meeting Preparation

- [ ] [ABAG] Bank selected and introduction arranged
- [ ] [ABAG] Meeting scheduled (in-person or video call)
- [ ] [CLIENT] All documents in Section A–D ready for the meeting
- [ ] [CLIENT] Authorized signatory available for the meeting
- [ ] [ABAG] Client briefed on expected questions and format
- [ ] [BOTH] Meeting format confirmed (in-person in Italy / video call / power of attorney)

---

## SECTION F — Post-Meeting Follow-Up

- [ ] [ABAG] All outstanding bank document requests fulfilled within 3 business days
- [ ] [ABAG] Bank internal KYC process monitored; chase every 5 business days if no update
- [ ] [CLIENT] Power of attorney signed if additional signatory authorization required
- [ ] [ABAG] IBAN confirmation received and shared with client

---

## SECTION G — Account Activation

- [ ] [CLIENT] Online banking activated — all authorized users have access
- [ ] [CLIENT] International wire transfer capability tested (test transfer from U.S. parent)
- [ ] [ABAG] Bank account details registered with Agenzia delle Entrate (if required for specific filings)
- [ ] [ABAG] Bank account integrated with accounting software
