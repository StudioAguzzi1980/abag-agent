# Checklist: New Client Onboarding

> **Instructions:** Use this checklist for every new ABAG client. Assign ownership for each item. Target completion of all items within the first 5 business days of the engagement.

---

## Day 1 — Engagement Confirmation

- [ ] Signed engagement letter received
- [ ] Initial fee payment received and confirmed
- [ ] New client record created in CRM
- [ ] Project manager assigned and notified
- [ ] Client file created in document management system
- [ ] Welcome email sent to client (see `/email_templates/onboarding_welcome.md`)

---

## Days 1–2 — Internal Setup

- [ ] Project kick-off scheduled with internal ABAG team
- [ ] Project plan created with milestones and deadlines
- [ ] Document checklist prepared for the client (based on services engaged)
- [ ] Compliance calendar started (if compliance services engaged)

---

## Days 2–3 — Client Introduction

- [ ] Project manager introduction call with client completed
- [ ] Document request sent to client with clear instructions
- [ ] ABAG contact sheet shared with client (PM email, phone, backup contacts)
- [ ] Client communication preferences confirmed (email, WhatsApp, Zoom, etc.)
- [ ] Client portal/document sharing system access granted

---

## Week 1 — Discovery and Data Gathering

- [ ] All required documents received from client (or outstanding items flagged)
- [ ] Discovery questionnaire completed (see `/client_onboarding/discovery_questionnaire.md`)
- [ ] Key dates confirmed: incorporation target, first employee hire date, banking urgency
- [ ] Tax structure confirmed with ABAG tax team
- [ ] U.S. CPA/advisor contact obtained and introductory email sent
- [ ] Billing details confirmed (invoicing address, EUR bank account or wire details)

---

## Week 2 — Service Commencement

- [ ] Service delivery confirmed started for each engaged service
- [ ] Any blockers identified and communicated to client
- [ ] First project status update sent to client
- [ ] Weekly update cadence confirmed

---

## Ongoing — Client Relationship Management

- [ ] Weekly project update sent (during active project phases)
- [ ] Monthly update sent (during retainer phase)
- [ ] Client satisfaction check-in at end of first project phase
- [ ] Upsell opportunity noted if additional services identified
