# Checklist: Italian Company Formation

> **Instructions:** Use this checklist to track progress through the Italian company formation process. Tick each item as it is completed. Share this checklist with the ABAG project team at the start of the engagement.

---

## Phase 1 — Pre-Formation Planning

- [ ] Discovery call completed with ABAG
- [ ] Entity type selected (S.r.l. / S.p.A. / Branch / Representative Office)
- [ ] Company name decided (check availability with Chamber of Commerce)
- [ ] Share capital amount agreed (minimum €10,000 for standard S.r.l.)
- [ ] Business purpose / ATECO code confirmed
- [ ] Registered office address confirmed (ABAG-provided or client's own)
- [ ] Fiscal year end confirmed (default: December 31)
- [ ] Director(s) identified: name, nationality, date of birth, passport details
- [ ] Shareholder(s) confirmed: individuals or corporate entities
- [ ] Engagement letter signed and initial fee paid

---

## Phase 2 — Document Collection

### From Individual Directors/Shareholders/Beneficial Owners

- [ ] Valid passport copy (color, signed page)
- [ ] Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement, <3 months old)
- [ ] Personal details form completed (ABAG template)
- [ ] Italian tax code (codice fiscale) — if already held

### From Corporate Shareholder (U.S. Company)

- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] Articles of Incorporation/Operating Agreement — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] Certificate of Good Standing — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] Incumbency Certificate or Officer Certificate — apostilled + certified Italian translation
- [ ] Board Resolution authorizing Italian company formation — apostilled (if required)
- [ ] Power of Attorney to ABAG (or Italian notary) — notarized + apostilled

---

## Phase 3 — Tax Code and Pre-Registration

- [ ] Codice fiscale applied for — all directors and shareholders
- [ ] Codice fiscale received and confirmed
- [ ] Company name confirmed as available in the Business Register
- [ ] Draft Atto Costitutivo and Statuto reviewed and approved by client
- [ ] Share capital deposit arranged (≥25% of share capital for standard S.r.l.)

---

## Phase 4 — Notarial Deed

- [ ] Notary appointment scheduled
- [ ] All documents confirmed ready for notary review
- [ ] Power of attorney verified by notary (if client not attending)
- [ ] Notarial deed executed
- [ ] Notary fees paid

---

## Phase 5 — Registration and Post-Formation

- [ ] Filing submitted to Chamber of Commerce by notary
- [ ] Business Register entry confirmed (Visura Camerale received)
- [ ] VAT number (Partita IVA) received from Agenzia delle Entrate
- [ ] Share capital fully deposited in corporate account (after account opening)

---

## Phase 6 — Banking (Parallel Track)

- [ ] Banking documentation package prepared (see `/checklists/banking_setup_checklist.md`)
- [ ] Bank selected and introductions arranged
- [ ] Bank meeting completed
- [ ] Account approved and IBAN received
- [ ] Online banking activated for all authorized signatories

---

## Phase 7 — Post-Formation Registrations

- [ ] INPS registration completed (if employing staff)
- [ ] INAIL registration completed (if employing staff)
- [ ] Registered office confirmed and operational
- [ ] Accounting system configured
- [ ] Electronic invoicing (SDI) system set up
- [ ] First VAT period calendar confirmed with ABAG

---

## Phase 8 — Completion Confirmation

- [ ] Final Visura Camerale shared with client
- [ ] VAT number confirmed active
- [ ] Partita IVA activation tested (first invoice issued)
- [ ] Bank account active
- [ ] Onboarding to accounting/compliance retainer confirmed
