# Checklist: Market Entry into Italy

> **Instructions:** This checklist is designed for U.S. companies planning their Italian market entry. Work through each section with your ABAG advisor. Not all items will apply to every client — mark N/A where appropriate.

---

## Phase 1 — Strategic Assessment

- [ ] Feasibility assessment completed (see `/market_entry/feasibility_assessment.md`)
- [ ] Market size and growth data collected for target sector
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapped — Italian, European, and other international competitors
- [ ] Target customer segments identified (B2B or B2C)
- [ ] Entry mode selected: subsidiary / distributor / agent / JV / representative office
- [ ] Key risks identified and mitigation strategies defined
- [ ] Go/No-Go decision made with senior management

---

## Phase 2 — Legal and Regulatory

- [ ] Italian regulatory requirements for your product/service confirmed
- [ ] Required licenses or permits identified
- [ ] EU product certifications confirmed (CE marking, food safety, novel food, etc.)
- [ ] Labeling requirements in Italian confirmed (language, mandatory elements)
- [ ] IP protection strategy confirmed (trademark registration in Italy/EU)
- [ ] Contract templates prepared (in Italian): terms of sale, NDA, distributor agreement
- [ ] GDPR compliance assessed for Italian operations

---

## Phase 3 — Legal Structure and Formation

- [ ] Entity type confirmed (S.r.l. / branch / representative office / distributor-only)
- [ ] Company formation initiated (see `/checklists/company_formation_checklist.md`)
- [ ] Registered office confirmed (ABAG-provided or own premises)
- [ ] Italian bank account initiated (see `/checklists/banking_setup_checklist.md`)

---

## Phase 4 — Tax and Finance

- [ ] Tax structure reviewed with ABAG tax team
- [ ] Transfer pricing approach documented (if intercompany transactions anticipated)
- [ ] Italian VAT registration obtained
- [ ] U.S.–Italy tax treaty analysis completed (withholding on dividends/royalties/interest)
- [ ] Available Italian tax incentives assessed (ZES, R&D credits, Transition 5.0)
- [ ] Year 1–3 financial model prepared (P&L, cash flow)
- [ ] Repatriation strategy confirmed (dividend timing, transfer pricing)

---

## Phase 5 — Commercial and Go-to-Market

- [ ] Go-to-market plan documented (see `/market_entry/go_to_market_strategy.md`)
- [ ] Target customer list (B2B) or target retail channels (B2C) confirmed
- [ ] Sales materials prepared in Italian (presentations, brochures, website)
- [ ] First Italian sales hire identified (internal or external)
- [ ] Distribution agreements reviewed and signed (if applicable)
- [ ] Pricing strategy for Italy confirmed (including channel margins and VAT)
- [ ] First Italian marketing campaign planned (LinkedIn, Google, trade events)

---

## Phase 6 — Operations

- [ ] Office space confirmed (if required) — lease signed or virtual office arranged
- [ ] IT and technology infrastructure operational in Italy
- [ ] Supply chain and logistics for Italian deliveries confirmed
- [ ] Italian suppliers or service providers engaged (as required)
- [ ] Business continuity plan for Italian operations documented

---

## Phase 7 — HR and Employment (if hiring)

- [ ] Italian employment structure decided (employee, secondee, contractor)
- [ ] Correct CCNL identified with ABAG
- [ ] First employment contracts drafted
- [ ] Centro per l'Impiego pre-hire notification process understood
- [ ] INPS and INAIL registrations completed
- [ ] Payroll processing arranged (ABAG or approved provider)
- [ ] Safety assessment (DVR) completed

---

## Phase 8 — Go-Live

- [ ] First Italian customer contract signed
- [ ] First Italian invoice issued and collected
- [ ] First month-end accounting close completed
- [ ] Monthly compliance routines confirmed with ABAG
