# Checklist: Tax Compliance — Italian Company

> **Instructions:** This checklist covers the key tax compliance requirements for a typical Italian S.r.l. Use in conjunction with the annual compliance calendar (`/checklists/annual_compliance_checklist.md`).

---

## VAT (IVA) Compliance

### Setup
- [ ] Partita IVA (VAT number) obtained at incorporation
- [ ] VAT filing frequency confirmed: monthly (above €400K revenue — goods / €700K — services) or quarterly
- [ ] Electronic invoicing (fatturazione elettronica via SDI) system configured
- [ ] Accounting software linked to SDI platform
- [ ] VAT rates correctly configured for each product/service category

### Monthly/Quarterly Compliance
- [ ] All sales invoices issued via SDI within 12 days of taxable event (standard B2B invoice)
- [ ] All purchase invoices received and recorded via SDI
- [ ] Monthly/quarterly VAT return (liquidazione IVA) prepared and filed
- [ ] Net VAT payment made via F24 by 16th of the following month (monthly filers)
- [ ] Quarterly VAT advance payment made (if applicable)

### Annual VAT
- [ ] Annual VAT return (dichiarazione IVA) filed by February 28

---

## Corporate Income Tax (IRES and IRAP)

### Setup
- [ ] Accounting system configured to track tax-deductible and non-deductible expenses
- [ ] Transfer pricing documentation prepared (if intercompany transactions exist)
- [ ] Italian tax code (codice fiscale) confirmed for all directors

### Ongoing
- [ ] First IRES/IRAP advance payment calculated and paid (June)
- [ ] Second IRES/IRAP advance payment calculated and paid (November)
- [ ] Annual IRES/IRAP return (Modello Redditi SC) filed by October 31

### Transfer Pricing (if applicable)
- [ ] Intercompany transactions documented before first transaction
- [ ] Transfer pricing method selected and documented
- [ ] Italian Local File prepared annually
- [ ] Master File maintained at group level (if required)
- [ ] Country-by-Country Report filed (if group revenue >€750M)

---

## Withholding Taxes

- [ ] All employment income subject to Italian withholding (ritenuta IRPEF) withheld and paid monthly
- [ ] Withholding on payments to non-resident entities (dividends, interest, royalties) calculated and withheld
- [ ] Treaty reduced rates applied where applicable and documentation maintained
- [ ] Annual withholding return (Modello 770) filed by October 31

---

## Payroll Tax and Social Security

- [ ] INPS contribution rates confirmed for applicable CCNL
- [ ] Monthly F24 payment filed and paid by 16th of the following month
- [ ] INAIL contributions paid annually (December)
- [ ] Annual 770 filed — Certificazione Unica (CU) issued to employees by March 31

---

## Cross-Border Tax Compliance

- [ ] Permanent establishment risk assessed for U.S. parent's Italian activities
- [ ] Intercompany transactions reviewed annually for arm's length compliance
- [ ] Withholding tax certifications (Certificazione Ritenute) issued to U.S. parent
- [ ] U.S.–Italy tax treaty benefit applications filed where applicable
- [ ] U.S. parent notified of Italian taxes paid (for foreign tax credit purposes)

---

## Italian Tax Incentives — Tracking

- [ ] R&D tax credit eligibility assessed annually
- [ ] Industria 4.0 / Transition 5.0 credits identified for qualifying investments
- [ ] Patent Box application status reviewed (if IP licensing income)
- [ ] ZES/SEZ credits claimed for qualifying Southern Italy investments (if applicable)
- [ ] ACE (equity deduction) calculated and applied (if still available under current rules)
