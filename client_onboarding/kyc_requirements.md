# Client KYC Requirements

## Overview

ABAG is subject to anti-money laundering (AML) obligations under Italian law (D.Lgs. 231/2007) and best professional practice. As part of onboarding every new client, ABAG must conduct Know Your Customer (KYC) due diligence to:

1. Verify the identity of all clients and beneficial owners
2. Understand the nature and purpose of the business relationship
3. Assess the AML risk profile of the engagement
4. Maintain records of all KYC documentation

Client cooperation in providing KYC information is essential. ABAG cannot commence services until the required KYC documentation has been received and verified.

---

## KYC Documentation Required

### For Individual Clients

| Document | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Valid government-issued photo ID | Passport (preferred); national ID card (as supplement) |
| Proof of residential address | Utility bill or bank statement; no older than 3 months |
| Tax identification | U.S. Social Security Number or EIN; Italian codice fiscale if available |
| Source of funds declaration | How was the capital invested in the Italian company accumulated? |
| PEP / Sanctions screening | ABAG will screen against relevant lists; no action required by client |

### For Corporate Clients

| Document | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Proof of incorporation | Certificate of Incorporation (apostilled) |
| Current organizational chart | Showing the full ownership chain to individual beneficial owners |
| Articles/Bylaws | Current version |
| Certificate of Good Standing | Issued within 6 months |
| Financial statements | Most recent year (audited, if available) |
| Identification of all beneficial owners with ≥25% ownership | As per individual requirements above |
| Identification of all directors and authorized representatives | As per individual requirements above |
| Business description | Nature of business, customers, jurisdictions of operation |

---

## Beneficial Owner Definition

Under Italian AML law, a "beneficial owner" (titolare effettivo) is:

- Any individual who **directly or indirectly owns or controls 25% or more** of the shares or voting rights in a legal entity
- Any individual who **otherwise controls** the entity (e.g., through special voting rights, contractual agreements, or management positions)
- For trusts: settlors, trustees, protectors, and beneficiaries

ABAG is required to identify and verify the identity of all beneficial owners.

---

## Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Additional documentation may be required in the following circumstances:
- Beneficial owners who are politically exposed persons (PEPs) or their close associates
- Clients with operations in or connections to high-risk jurisdictions
- Complex ownership structures involving multiple jurisdictions
- High-value transactions or unusually large capital contributions
- Any circumstances that raise AML risk concerns

ABAG will notify the client if EDD is required and explain what additional information is needed.

---

## Confidentiality

All KYC information provided to ABAG is:
- Held strictly confidentially in accordance with ABAG's privacy policy
- Used solely for the purposes of KYC/AML compliance and service delivery
- Retained for the legally required period (minimum 10 years under Italian AML law)
- Never shared with third parties except as required by law or regulation
