# Client Onboarding — Overview

## Purpose

This section contains all materials related to onboarding new ABAG clients — from the moment an engagement letter is signed through to the point where the client is fully set up and receiving ongoing services.

A professional, smooth onboarding experience sets the tone for the entire client relationship. It builds trust, demonstrates ABAG's organizational capability, and reduces the risk of misunderstandings or delays.

---

## Onboarding Process Overview

```
Engagement Letter Signed + Fee Received
              ↓
Welcome Email Sent (Day 1)
              ↓
Internal Project Setup (Days 1–2)
              ↓
Project Manager Introduction Call (Days 2–3)
              ↓
Document Request Sent to Client (Day 2–3)
              ↓
Discovery Questionnaire Completed (Week 1)
              ↓
Document Collection Complete (Week 2–3)
              ↓
Service Delivery Commenced
              ↓
Ongoing Service and Account Management
```

## Key Principles

1. **Speed** — Begin onboarding within 24 hours of the engagement letter being signed
2. **Clarity** — Every client should know exactly what is happening, when, and who is responsible
3. **Proactivity** — Don't wait for the client to chase; ABAG leads the process
4. **Documentation** — All client information and documents must be properly filed and organized
5. **Communication** — Weekly updates during active phases; monthly during steady-state

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Sales/Business Development** | Handover client information to project team at signing |
| **Project Manager** | Own the entire onboarding process; primary client contact |
| **Tax Team** | Conduct tax structure review and provide guidance on formation decisions |
| **Compliance Team** | Set up compliance calendar and KYC review |
| **Accounts/Finance** | Issue first invoice; confirm payment received |
