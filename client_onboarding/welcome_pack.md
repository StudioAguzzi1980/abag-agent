## 8. Important Reminders

- **All communications with Italian authorities will be in Italian** — ABAG handles this; no Italian required from you
- **ABAG acts under your power of attorney** for most Italian filings — please execute and return the PoA as instructed
- **Please respond promptly to document requests** — each delay in document submission extends the timeline proportionally
- **Costs are invoiced transparently** — all third-party costs (government fees, notary, translations) are charged at actual cost with supporting receipts
