# Company Formation Costs and Fees

## Overview

This document provides a breakdown of the typical costs involved in forming an Italian company. Costs fall into two categories: **professional fees** (ABAG's charges) and **third-party/government fees** (notary, registration fees, translations, etc.).

> **Note:** All figures below are indicative. Actual costs depend on the specific circumstances, chosen structure, and current fee schedules. Contact ABAG for a precise quote.

---

## S.r.l. Formation — Cost Breakdown

### ABAG Professional Fees

| Service | Indicative Range |
|---|---|
| Company formation management (full service) | €2,500 – €5,000 |
| Registered office provision (per year) | €800 – €2,400 |
| Post-incorporation setup support | Included or €500 – €1,000 |

### Third-Party and Government Fees

| Item | Indicative Amount |
|---|---|
| Italian notary fees (including government taxes) | €1,500 – €3,000 |
| Chamber of Commerce registration fee | €150 – €250 |
| Business Register annual fee (diritto annuale) | €100 – €200/year |
| Codice fiscale applications | Minimal (included in ABAG fee) |
| VAT registration (Partita IVA) | Government fee — included in above |
| Apostille fees (U.S. state) | $20–$200 per document (varies by state) |
| Certified Italian translation | €200 – €600 per document |

### Total Estimated Cost — Standard S.r.l.

| Component | Estimate |
|---|---|
| ABAG professional fees | €2,500 – €5,000 |
| Third-party and government fees | €2,000 – €4,000 |
| **Total** | **€4,500 – €9,000** |

*Share capital of minimum €2,500 (25% of €10,000 standard minimum) must also be deposited, though this is the company's own capital — not a cost.*

## Payment Schedule (Typical)

For ABAG's formation service:
- **50%** of professional fees due upon signing the engagement letter
- **50%** upon completion of Chamber of Commerce registration
- Third-party costs invoiced at cost with supporting receipts

## VAT on ABAG's Invoices

ABAG's invoices are issued in EUR. VAT treatment:
- For Italian-registered entities: Italian VAT (22%) applies
- For non-EU clients: Generally no Italian VAT (reverse charge or outside scope — confirm with ABAG)
