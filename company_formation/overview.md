# Company Formation in Italy — Overview

## Introduction

Establishing a legal entity in Italy is the foundational step for any U.S. company seeking to operate, generate revenue, or employ staff in Italy. The process involves interactions with Italian notaries, the Chamber of Commerce, the tax authority (Agenzia delle Entrate), and social security institutions (INPS, INAIL).

ABAG manages this process end-to-end, ensuring that the correct structure is selected, all documentation is in order, and every registration step is completed efficiently.

---

## Available Legal Structures

| Structure | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **S.r.l.** | Società a Responsabilità Limitata — Italian LLC | Most foreign-owned businesses; SMEs to multinationals |
| **S.r.l.s.** | Simplified S.r.l. — low capital, restricted transferability | Micro-businesses and startups; not suitable for most foreign investments |
| **S.p.A.** | Società per Azioni — Joint-stock company | Large enterprises; companies planning external investment or listing |
| **Branch Office** | Sede Secondaria — extension of foreign parent | Testing the market; parent bears full liability |
| **Representative Office** | Ufficio di Rappresentanza — non-commercial presence | Pre-launch research and liaison; cannot generate revenue |

---

## Choice of Structure — Key Factors

The right structure depends on:

1. **Liability** — Does the parent company want to limit exposure? (Choose S.r.l. or S.p.A.)
2. **Scale** — Is this a full subsidiary or a lightweight market presence? (S.r.l. vs. representative office)
3. **Revenue generation** — Will the Italian entity invoice customers directly? (Subsidiary required)
4. **Employees** — Will the Italian entity employ staff? (Subsidiary preferred; branch possible)
5. **Capital** — How much capital needs to be invested? (S.p.A. requires €50,000 minimum)
6. **Exit options** — Is there a potential sale or restructuring in the future? (S.r.l. has flexible transfer mechanics)

For the vast majority of U.S. companies, an **S.r.l.** is the recommended starting structure.

## Formation Process at a Glance

```
Decision on structure
        ↓
Obtain codice fiscale for directors/shareholders
        ↓
Prepare articles of incorporation and bylaws
        ↓
Notarial deed before Italian notary
        ↓
Register with Chamber of Commerce
        ↓
Obtain VAT number (Partita IVA)
        ↓
Open corporate bank account
        ↓
Register for INPS/INAIL (if employing staff)
        ↓
Obtain sector licenses (if required)
        ↓
Operational
```
