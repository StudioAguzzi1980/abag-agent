# Complete Guide to S.r.l. Formation in Italy

## What Is an S.r.l.?

The **Società a Responsabilità Limitata (S.r.l.)** is Italy's most widely used corporate structure for small to mid-size and foreign-owned businesses. It is broadly equivalent to a U.S. LLC or a UK private limited company.

Key characteristics:
- Shareholders' liability is **limited to their capital contribution**
- The S.r.l. is a **separate legal entity** from its shareholders
- Managed by one or more **directors (amministratori)**, appointed by shareholders
- Shares (quote) are **not publicly tradeable** (unlike S.p.A.)
- Flexible governance and relatively low capital requirements

---

## Types of S.r.l.

| Type | Min. Share Capital | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard S.r.l.** | €10,000 | Most foreign-owned businesses |
| **Simplified S.r.l. (S.r.l.s.)** | €1 (up to €9,999) | Italian residents only; not suitable for foreign corporate shareholders |
| **Innovative Startup S.r.l.** | €1 | Tech startups meeting qualifying criteria |

For U.S. clients, the **Standard S.r.l.** is almost always the appropriate choice.

## Step-by-Step Formation Process

### Step 1 — Preliminary Planning

Before any documentation is prepared, confirm:
- [ ] Company name (check availability with the Chamber of Commerce)
- [ ] Share capital amount and initial shareholder(s)
- [ ] Director(s): name(s), nationality, place and date of birth
- [ ] Registered office address in Italy
- [ ] Business activity (ATECO code — the Italian industry classification)
- [ ] Fiscal year end

### Step 2 — Obtain Italian Tax Codes (Codice Fiscale)

All directors, shareholders (individuals), and beneficial owners must obtain an Italian tax code (codice fiscale) from the Agenzia delle Entrate.

**For non-resident individuals:**
- ABAG can apply on behalf of clients by submitting passport copies and personal details
- Processing time: approximately 5–10 business days
- The codice fiscale is a 16-character alphanumeric code unique to each individual

**For U.S. corporate shareholders:**
- The U.S. company must obtain its own Italian tax code
- Requires submitting apostilled and translated corporate documents

### Step 3 — Prepare Corporate Documents

ABAG drafts:
- **Atto Costitutivo** (articles of incorporation): establishes the company, lists shareholders and capital contributions
- **Statuto** (company bylaws): governs the company's operations, director powers, profit distribution, and share transfer rules

These documents are prepared in **Italian**, as required by Italian law.

### Step 4 — Power of Attorney (if applicable)

If the shareholders and directors cannot attend in person for the notarial deed:
- A **Power of Attorney (Procura)** authorizing ABAG (or an Italian professional) to sign the incorporation deed on their behalf must be prepared, **notarized**, and **apostilled** in the U.S.
- ABAG provides a template and detailed instructions for notarization and apostille

**Notarization process for U.S. clients:**
1. Sign the Power of Attorney before a U.S. notary public
2. Have the notary's signature authenticated by the county clerk (if required in your state)
3. Obtain an apostille from your state's Secretary of State office
4. Send the original to ABAG in Italy

### Step 5 — Capital Deposit

For standard S.r.l., at least **25% of the share capital** must be deposited in a dedicated bank account or with a notary before the deed is signed. ABAG facilitates this via a temporary deposit account.

### Step 6 — Notarial Deed

The incorporation deed is executed before an **Italian notary (notaio)**. If ABAG holds a power of attorney, the client does not need to be present.

**The notarial deed includes:**
- Company name and registered office
- Shareholders and their capital contributions
- Director(s) appointed at incorporation
- Business purpose (oggetto sociale)
- Duration of the company

The notary then submits the deed to the Chamber of Commerce for registration.

### Step 7 — Chamber of Commerce Registration

The notary files the incorporation deed with the relevant **Camera di Commercio** (Chamber of Commerce). The company is entered in the **Registro delle Imprese** (Business Register), which generates:
- The company's fiscal code and VAT number (or these may be applied for separately)
- The **Visura Camerale** — the official Business Register extract

**Timeline:** Typically 7–15 business days from notarial deed.

### Step 8 — VAT Registration (Partita IVA)

ABAG submits the VAT registration application to the **Agenzia delle Entrate** (or it is processed as part of the Chamber of Commerce filing for some entity types). The company receives its VAT number (Partita IVA), which is 11 digits and begins with "IT".

### Step 9 — Open Corporate Bank Account

After receiving the Visura Camerale and VAT number, ABAG assists in opening the corporate bank account (see `/banking/opening_corporate_account.md`).

### Step 10 — INPS Registration

If the company will employ staff, ABAG registers the company with:
- **INPS** (National Social Security Institute)
- **INAIL** (National Institute for Workplace Accident Insurance)

## Governance Structure

### Shareholders (Soci)
- Can be individuals or legal entities
- Minimum 1 shareholder (Italian law permits single-member S.r.l.)
- Decisions made at shareholders' meetings or via written resolutions (where permitted by bylaws)

### Directors (Amministratori)
- Minimum 1 director; can also be a sole director (amministratore unico)
- No requirement to be Italian residents or citizens
- Must have an Italian tax code (codice fiscale)
- Directors are appointed (and removed) by shareholders
- A **Board of Directors (Consiglio di Amministrazione)** can be established for larger companies

### Statutory Auditors
- Generally not required for smaller S.r.l.
- Mandatory if two of three thresholds are exceeded for two consecutive years: total assets > €4.4M; revenues > €8.8M; employees > 50

## Ongoing Obligations After Formation

| Obligation | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Annual accounts approval (shareholders' meeting) | Annual (within 120–180 days of year-end) |
| File accounts with Chamber of Commerce | Annual (within 30 days of approval) |
| VAT returns | Monthly or quarterly |
| IRES/IRAP advance payments | June and November |
| Annual IRES/IRAP return | Within 11 months of year-end |
| Update Business Register for corporate changes | Within 30 days of any change |
