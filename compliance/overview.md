# Corporate Compliance — Overview

## Introduction

Operating an Italian company involves a continuous stream of regulatory, legal, and governance obligations. These obligations span corporate law, tax law, employment law, data protection, and anti-money laundering — and they do not pause between deadlines.

Non-compliance carries real financial consequences (administrative penalties, fines, interest) and reputational risks. Directors can bear personal liability in some circumstances.

ABAG's compliance service ensures that every obligation is tracked, prepared, and filed on time — giving clients complete peace of mind about their Italian regulatory standing.

## Compliance Obligations by Category

### 1. Corporate Governance and Business Register

| Obligation | Frequency | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| Hold annual shareholders' meeting to approve accounts | Annual | Company |
| File annual accounts with Business Register | Annual | ABAG |
| Update Business Register for corporate changes | Within 30 days of change | ABAG |
| Maintain minute book (libro dei verbali) | Ongoing | ABAG |
| Prepare board and shareholder resolutions | As needed | ABAG |
| Beneficial ownership declaration (Titolare Effettivo) | Annual / on change | ABAG |

## Annual Compliance Calendar

Key deadlines for Italian companies with a December 31 fiscal year-end:

| Month | Key Obligation |
|---|---|
| January | Annual VAT declaration preparation begins |
| February | Annual VAT declaration filing deadline (Feb 28) |
| March | Issue CU forms to employees (March 31) |
| April | First quarterly VAT return (Q1) |
| May | Shareholders' meeting convened (within 120 days = April 30 for Dec year-end; up to 180 days = June 30 in some cases) |
| June | Approve annual accounts; IRES/IRAP first advance payment; annual accounts filing with Chamber of Commerce |
| July | Second quarterly VAT return (Q2) |
| October | File annual IRES/IRAP return (October 31 deadline) |
| October | Third quarterly VAT return (Q3) |
| November | IRES/IRAP second advance payment |
| December | Annual accounting close; review GDPR and AML documentation; update Business Register if needed |
