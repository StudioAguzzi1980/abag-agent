# Documents Section — README

## Overview

This section contains templates and guides for the key legal and business documents used in ABAG engagements and by ABAG's clients operating in Italy.

> **Important Legal Disclaimer:** All document templates in this section are provided for general guidance and reference purposes only. They are not legal advice. Every document used in a real transaction must be reviewed and finalized by a qualified Italian lawyer or legal professional. ABAG recommends using these templates as a starting point and always engaging appropriate legal counsel before executing binding documents.

## Document Preparation Notes

### Language Requirements

- Documents to be filed with Italian authorities must be in Italian
- Commercial contracts with Italian counterparties are typically in Italian or bilingual (Italian/English)
- ABAG can prepare bilingual templates on request

### Legal Review

- All contracts should be reviewed by a qualified Italian lawyer before execution
- Template documents in this section may not reflect the most current Italian law
- ABAG coordinates legal review for clients on request

### Execution Formalities

Some Italian documents require specific execution formalities:
- **Contracts above certain value thresholds** may require written form
- **Property-related contracts** typically require notarial execution
- **Powers of attorney** for Italian use typically require notarization and apostille (for U.S. parties)
- **Contracts with employees** must meet specific Italian labor law requirements

## How to Use These Templates

1. Review the template and understand its structure
2. Replace all `[BRACKETED]` fields with the relevant information
3. Have the document reviewed by a qualified Italian lawyer
4. Execute following the required formalities
5. Store the executed document securely
