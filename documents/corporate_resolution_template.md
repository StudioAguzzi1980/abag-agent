# Corporate Resolution Templates — Italian S.r.l.

> **Note:** These templates are for reference purposes. Actual resolutions must be prepared in Italian, reflect the specific facts of each decision, and comply with the company's bylaws (Statuto). ABAG prepares resolutions as part of its compliance service.

---

## Template 1 — Shareholders' Resolution to Open a Bank Account

**DECISIONE DEI SOCI**
**[COMPANY NAME] S.r.l.**
**Codice Fiscale / P.IVA: [NUMBER]**

---

**Date:** [DATE]

The sole shareholder / The shareholders of [COMPANY NAME] S.r.l. [having met on the above date at the registered office / acting pursuant to Article [X] of the company bylaws by written resolution without a meeting], hereby unanimously resolve:

1. To authorize the opening of a corporate current account at [BANK NAME], [BRANCH ADDRESS] in the name of [COMPANY NAME] S.r.l.

2. To authorize the following individuals to operate the account, each acting individually / jointly:
   - [NAME], [TITLE], born in [PLACE] on [DATE], codice fiscale [CF]
   - [NAME], [TITLE], born in [PLACE] on [DATE], codice fiscale [CF]

3. The authorized persons are empowered to sign all documents, mandates, and forms required by the bank in connection with the operation of the above account.

Signed:

[SHAREHOLDER NAME] — [Sole Shareholder / Shareholder — [X]% stake]

[PLACE], [DATE]

## Template 2 — Board Resolution to Appoint a Director

**VERBALE DI ASSEMBLEA DEI SOCI / DECISIONE DEI SOCI**
**[COMPANY NAME] S.r.l.**

**Date:** [DATE]

The shareholders of [COMPANY NAME] S.r.l., representing [100%] of the share capital, hereby resolve:

1. To appoint [DIRECTOR FULL NAME], born in [PLACE] on [DATE], codice fiscale [CF], residing at [ADDRESS], as [Sole Director (Amministratore Unico) / Director (Amministratore)] of [COMPANY NAME] S.r.l. with immediate effect.

2. The Director shall hold office [for an indefinite period / until [DATE]], subject to early revocation by the shareholders.

3. The Director shall be entitled to represent the company with full powers, with the right to sign all documents, contracts, and official correspondence on behalf of the company, within the limits set by the bylaws.

4. The Director's compensation shall be [€[AMOUNT] per [year/month] / zero / as separately agreed in writing].

Signed:

[SHAREHOLDER NAME]

[PLACE], [DATE]

## Template 3 — Shareholders' Resolution to Approve Annual Accounts

**VERBALE DI ASSEMBLEA ORDINARIA DEI SOCI**
**[COMPANY NAME] S.r.l.**

**Date:** [DATE]
**Location:** [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS / remote by video conference]
**Present:** [NAMES OF SHAREHOLDERS / Sole Shareholder]
**Chairperson:** [NAME]

The shareholders, representing [100%] of the share capital, convened in ordinary assembly and:

1. Examined the financial statements (bilancio d'esercizio) for the year ended December 31, [YEAR], consisting of the balance sheet, income statement, and notes to the accounts, together with the directors' report.

2. **Resolved to approve** the financial statements for the year ended December 31, [YEAR], as presented, reflecting:
   - Total assets: €[AMOUNT]
   - Net revenue: €[AMOUNT]
   - Net profit/(loss): €[AMOUNT]

3. **Resolved** that the net profit/(loss) for the year of €[AMOUNT] shall be [carried forward / allocated to reserves / distributed as follows: [describe]].

4. No further business.

Chairperson's signature:

[NAME]

[PLACE], [DATE]

## Notes on Italian Corporate Resolutions

- Resolutions must be recorded in the **Libro dei Verbali** (Minute Book)
- Resolutions in Italian are required for filing with the Business Register
- Certain resolutions require filing with the Chamber of Commerce within 30 days
- ABAG prepares all routine resolutions as part of the compliance retainer
- Extraordinary resolutions (capital increases, mergers, amendments to bylaws) typically require notarial intervention
