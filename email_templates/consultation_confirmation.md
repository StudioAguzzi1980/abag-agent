**Before Our Call**

To make the most of our time together, it would be helpful if you could give us a brief overview of your situation in advance. Even a few sentences about the following would be valuable:

- Your business and what you're working on
- Your primary goal or question regarding Italy
- Your approximate timeline

You can reply to this email or simply bring these points to the call — whatever is easier.

---

**What to Expect**

This is a discovery conversation, not a sales presentation. We want to understand your situation and provide genuinely useful information. By the end of the call, you'll have:

- A clearer picture of the process involved in your specific case
- An understanding of what ABAG can do for you
- A concrete suggested next step (which may or may not involve a formal engagement)

There is no obligation to proceed with anything following the consultation.
