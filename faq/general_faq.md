# FAQ — General Questions About ABAG

## About ABAG

**Q: What is ABAG?**
A: American Business Advisory Group (ABAG) is a specialist advisory firm dedicated to helping U.S. companies and entrepreneurs establish, operate, and grow in Italy and Europe. We provide company formation, tax advisory, accounting, compliance, banking support, and market entry services — all with a focus on the American client experience.

---

**Q: Where is ABAG located?**
A: ABAG operates across Italy with a focus on supporting U.S.-based clients remotely. The majority of our work is conducted without requiring clients to travel to Italy. Contact us at [abag.us](https://abag.us) for current office locations.

---

**Q: Who are ABAG's typical clients?**
A: Our clients include:
- U.S. entrepreneurs and small business owners expanding to Italy
- U.S. mid-size and large corporations establishing Italian subsidiaries
- American professionals relocating to Italy who need to structure their business
- U.S. investors with Italian portfolio companies
- International companies using Italy as a European base

---

**Q: What makes ABAG different from a standard Italian advisory firm?**
A: Most Italian advisory firms are excellent within Italian law but have limited experience with U.S. business structures, U.S. GAAP reporting, or the cross-border tax implications for American clients. ABAG's team understands both sides — Italian regulatory depth combined with genuine fluency in U.S. business culture and requirements.

---

**Q: Does ABAG handle all aspects of Italian market entry, or do I need multiple advisors?**
A: ABAG is a single point of contact for the vast majority of what U.S. companies need to operate in Italy — company formation, tax, accounting, compliance, and banking. For areas outside our core scope (e.g., Italian litigation, real estate, sector-specific licensing), we refer clients to trusted specialists within our professional network.

---

**Q: Does ABAG work in Italian and English?**
A: Yes. ABAG works fluently in both languages. All client communication is conducted in English; all Italian authorities and counterparties are engaged in Italian. Clients never need to navigate Italian-language bureaucracy directly.

---

**Q: How does ABAG charge for its services?**
A: ABAG charges through a mix of fixed-fee project engagements (e.g., company formation, banking support) and monthly retainers (e.g., accounting, compliance monitoring). A detailed fee breakdown is provided in every formal proposal. See also `/services/pricing_guide.md`.

---

**Q: How do I get started with ABAG?**
A: The first step is a discovery consultation — a 30–45 minute call to understand your situation and objectives. There is no obligation following the consultation. You can schedule a call at [abag.us](https://abag.us) or by contacting our team directly.

---

**Q: Does ABAG provide legal advice?**
A: ABAG provides business advisory services, including guidance on Italian regulatory and compliance matters. Where specific legal advice is required (e.g., drafting of commercial contracts, litigation strategy, employment disputes), we engage or refer specialist Italian lawyers within our network.

---

**Q: Can ABAG help with countries other than Italy?**
A: ABAG's primary expertise is Italy. For broader European expansion beyond Italy, we can provide strategic guidance and refer clients to trusted advisors in other EU jurisdictions. Italy is often used as a gateway to the broader EU single market.
