# Italian Corporate Tax — IRES and IRAP

## IRES — Imposta sul Reddito delle Società (Corporate Income Tax)

### What Is IRES?

IRES is Italy's corporate income tax, levied on the taxable profits of:
- Italian resident companies (corporations, S.r.l., S.p.A., cooperatives)
- Permanent establishments of foreign companies in Italy
- Other entities with a corporate legal form

### IRES Rate

The standard IRES rate is **24%**.

A reduced rate of **3.5%** applies to certain cooperative entities — this is not relevant for standard S.r.l./S.p.A. entities.

### Taxable Base

IRES is applied to **taxable income**, which is the company's net accounting profit adjusted for tax purposes:

- **Starting point:** Net profit per Italian GAAP (or IFRS for qualifying entities)
- **Add back:** Non-deductible expenses (entertainment, certain provisions, etc.)
- **Deduct:** Tax-exempt income, accelerated depreciation, ACE deduction, and other adjustments
- **Result:** Taxable income (reddito d'impresa imponibile)

### Deductibility Rules

Key deductibility rules under Italian tax law:

| Item | Deductibility |
|---|---|
| Employee costs | Fully deductible (with some exceptions for high earners) |
| Depreciation | Deductible at statutory rates (see Ministerial Decree tables) |
| Interest expense | Deductible up to 30% of EBITDA (interest deduction limitation — Art. 96 TUIR) |
| R&D expenditure | Deductible; eligible for additional credit |
| Representation expenses | Limited to 1.5% of revenues |
| Provisions | Generally not deductible until realized |
| Intercompany transactions | Deductible if at arm's length |

### IRES Payment Calendar

| Payment | Deadline | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| First advance (acconto) | June 30 | 40% of prior year liability |
| Second advance | November 30 | 60% of prior year liability |
| Balance (saldo) | June 30 following year | Remaining amount due after return |

## IRAP — Imposta Regionale sulle Attività Produttive (Regional Production Tax)

### What Is IRAP?

IRAP is a regional tax levied on the "net production value" of business activities carried out in Italy. Unlike IRES, IRAP is **not a tax on profit** — it is levied on a broader value-added base that includes employee costs and other items not deductible from IRES.

### IRAP Rate

The standard IRAP rate is **3.9%**, but rates vary slightly by region:
- Some regions apply surcharges (up to +0.92%)
- Some regions apply reductions for qualifying activities or investments

IRAP is administered regionally, with returns filed to the regional authority.

### IRAP Taxable Base

The IRAP base is the **net production value** (valore della produzione netta), calculated broadly as:
- Revenue
- Minus: Cost of goods sold and services (excluding labor)
- **Note:** Employee costs are generally **not deductible** from the IRAP base (unlike IRES)
- Certain exceptions apply for certain categories of employment contracts

This means IRAP can be significant even for companies with modest profits — because the tax base is revenue-minus-non-labor costs, not net profit.

### IRAP and Holding Companies

Holding companies that earn only dividends and capital gains may argue that they do not conduct a "productive activity" (attività produttiva) in Italy and therefore fall outside the IRAP scope. This is a complex area — specialist advice is required.

### IRAP Payment Calendar

Same as IRES:
- First advance: June 30
- Second advance: November 30
- Balance: June 30 following year

## Combined Tax Rate Planning

The combined IRES + IRAP tax burden depends heavily on the business model:
- For **labor-intensive businesses**, IRAP effectively increases the total tax rate significantly (since wages are not deductible from the IRAP base)
- For **capital-intensive businesses** with few employees, the combined effective rate is closer to the 24% IRES rate
- **Tax planning** can legitimately manage both taxes through structure, depreciation strategies, ACE deductions, and incentive programs

## Key Tax Incentives Affecting IRES

| Incentive | Impact on IRES |
|---|---|
| **Patent Box** | 50% partial exemption on qualifying IP income |
| **ACE** | Notional interest deduction on equity increases |
| **R&D Tax Credit** | Tax credit (20–45%) on qualifying R&D spend |
| **Industria 4.0 / Transition 5.0** | Investment tax credit on qualifying capital expenditure |
| **ZES (South Italy)** | Tax credit of up to 50% on qualifying investment |
