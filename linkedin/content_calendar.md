# LinkedIn Content Calendar

## Overview

This content calendar provides a 4-week rolling framework for ABAG's LinkedIn posting strategy. Content is structured around the five core pillars defined in the Content Strategy, with variety across formats to maximize engagement.

---

## Posting Frequency

- **Company Page:** 3–5 posts per week
- **Key Team Members:** 3–5 posts per week (personal profiles amplify company reach)
- **Best posting times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10 AM CET (peak engagement for professional audiences in Europe and overlapping with U.S. morning)

---

## Content Mix (Per 4-Week Cycle)

| Format | Frequency | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Educational post (tip or explainer) | 6–8 | Italian Business 101, Tax & Compliance |
| Carousel / slide post | 4 | Market Entry, Banking |
| Case study or client story (anonymized) | 1–2 | ABAG Expertise |
| Market insight / news comment | 2–4 | Market Entry, Tax |
