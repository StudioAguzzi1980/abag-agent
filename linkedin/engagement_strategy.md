# LinkedIn Engagement Strategy

## Overview

LinkedIn is ABAG's primary digital marketing and business development channel. This document outlines a systematic approach to building presence, generating engagement, and converting LinkedIn activity into qualified leads.

---

## Goals

1. **Brand awareness** — Ensure ABAG is recognized as the go-to resource for U.S. companies looking to enter Italy
2. **Thought leadership** — Position ABAG team members as credible, knowledgeable advisors in the field
3. **Lead generation** — Convert profile visits and post engagement into discovery calls and qualified opportunities
4. **Referral network development** — Build relationships with U.S. law firms, CPAs, investment banks, and other referral sources

---

## Profile Engagement Strategy

### Step 1 — Build the Audience Foundation

**Target connections to prioritize:**
- U.S. entrepreneurs and founders in relevant industries
- Corporate development and international strategy executives at U.S. companies
- U.S.-based CPAs and tax advisors with international clients
- U.S. law firms with cross-border practices
- Italian Chamber of Commerce contacts and trade association members
- Private equity and family office professionals

**Connection targets per month:** 50–100 new targeted connections per active team member

**Process:**
1. Search LinkedIn for target profiles using filters (title, location, industry)
2. Send personalized connection requests (see `/linkedin/outreach_templates.md`)
3. Follow up with a first message within 48 hours of connection acceptance
4. Log all outreach in CRM

---

## Content Engagement Strategy

### Engage Before Publishing

Before posting content, spend 15–20 minutes engaging with other people's content:
- Like and comment meaningfully on posts from target personas
- Comment on posts from Italian business / regulatory sources
- Share relevant articles with ABAG's perspective added

This warms the algorithm and builds reciprocal engagement.

### Respond to Every Comment

- Respond to every comment on ABAG posts within 2–4 hours during business hours
- Ask follow-up questions to deepen the conversation
- Engage with the profiles of users who comment — like their recent posts

### Amplify with Personal Profiles

- Team members should reshare company page posts with personalized commentary
- This dramatically increases organic reach
- Assign a team member to reshare each company post within 2–4 hours of publishing

---

## Hashtag and Algorithm Strategy

**Algorithm factors that increase reach:**
- Engagement in the first 60–90 minutes after posting (likes, comments)
- Long dwell time (carousels and polls perform well)
- External link in comment, not in post body (to avoid algorithm penalty)
- Native content (upload video directly vs. YouTube link)

**Hashtag best practice:**
- Use 3–5 hashtags per post (not more)
- Mix: 1 large (#Business, #Italy — 100K+ followers), 2 mid (#ItalyBusiness, #MarketEntry — 10K–100K), 1 niche (#USCompaniesItaly, #ABAGAdvisory)

## LinkedIn Newsletter

Launch an ABAG LinkedIn Newsletter on a topic like:
> **"Italy Business Insider"** — Monthly insights for U.S. companies operating in or entering Italy

**Content:** Regulatory updates, market insights, tips, case study snippets

**Goal:** Build a subscriber base that receives each newsletter as a direct notification, independently of the feed algorithm

---

## LinkedIn Events

Use LinkedIn Events for webinars and live sessions:

1. Create event 2–4 weeks in advance
2. Invite relevant connections personally
3. Promote event in posts leading up to the date
4. Go live or share recorded replay post-event
5. Follow up with attendees and no-shows separately

## Tracking and Measurement

Track the following metrics monthly:

| Metric | Source | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Follower growth (company page) | LinkedIn Analytics | +50–100/month |
| Post impressions | LinkedIn Analytics | Track trend |
| Average engagement rate | LinkedIn Analytics | ≥ 3% |
| Profile views (per team member) | LinkedIn Dashboard | Track trend |
| Connection requests accepted | CRM | ≥ 70% acceptance rate |
| Discovery calls from LinkedIn | CRM | Track and optimize |
| Deals sourced from LinkedIn | CRM | Track monthly |

## Monthly LinkedIn Routine

| Activity | Frequency | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Targeted connection outreach | Daily | 15–20 min |
| Content engagement (comments on others) | Daily | 15–20 min |
| Company page post | 4–5x/week | 30 min |
| Personal post (team members) | 3–4x/week | 20 min |
| Respond to comments/messages | Daily | 15–30 min |
| Review analytics and adjust strategy | Monthly | 1 hour |

*LinkedIn strategy should be reviewed quarterly and adjusted based on platform changes and performance data.*
