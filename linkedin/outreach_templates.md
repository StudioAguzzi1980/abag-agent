# LinkedIn Outreach Templates

## Purpose

These templates are designed for personalized outreach on LinkedIn to prospective clients and referral partners. Always customize before sending — generic messages are low-performing. Use these as starting points, not copy-paste messages.

---

## Outreach Category 1 — U.S. Entrepreneurs / Founders

### Template A — Connection Request (Note)
> "Hi [NAME], I saw your post about [expanding to Italy / looking into European markets / your business in Italy]. I'm with ABAG — we help U.S. companies establish and operate in Italy. Happy to connect and share some insights if it's helpful."

### Template B — First Message After Connection
> "Hi [NAME], thanks for connecting.
>
> I noticed [you mentioned expanding to Italy / you run [type of business] which fits well with what we see in the Italian market]. ABAG specializes in helping American entrepreneurs like yourself navigate the Italian setup process — company formation, tax, banking, and ongoing compliance.
>
> I'm not going to push a sales pitch, but I'm happy to answer any questions you have about Italy as a market, or point you in the right direction. What's your timeline looking like for this?"

### Template C — Follow-Up (No Response After 7 Days)
> "Hi [NAME], just circling back on my previous message in case it got buried. No pressure at all — just wanted to make sure it reached you. If you're still exploring Italy, I'd be happy to have a quick call. Otherwise, feel free to reach out whenever the time is right."

## Outreach Category 3 — Referral Partners (U.S. Law Firms, CPAs, Consultants)

### Template A — Connection Request (Note)
> "Hi [NAME], I work with ABAG — we specialize in Italian market entry for U.S. companies. Thought it could be worth connecting given your work in [international tax / corporate law / international expansion]. Happy to be a resource for your clients with Italy questions."

> "Hi [NAME], thank you for connecting.
>
> I wanted to introduce ABAG as a potential resource for your clients. We're a specialist advisory firm focused exclusively on helping U.S. companies set up and operate in Italy — company formation, tax, banking, compliance, and market entry.
>
> We work well alongside U.S. law firms and accounting practices as the Italy-specific layer. Several of our client relationships began through exactly this kind of professional referral.
>
> If any of your clients ever have Italian market questions, I'm happy to be a resource — no strings attached. And if there are situations where a formal referral arrangement makes sense, I'm open to discussing that too.
>
> Would you be open to a brief call to learn more about what we do?"

## Key Principles for LinkedIn Outreach

1. **Personalize every message** — Reference their name, company, role, or something specific from their profile
2. **Lead with value** — Don't open with "Can I get 15 minutes of your time?" — offer something useful first
3. **Keep it short** — No message longer than 5–6 sentences in the first contact
4. **One clear ask per message** — Don't ask multiple questions or propose multiple next steps
5. **Follow up once** — A single follow-up is appropriate; multiple follow-ups damage trust
6. **Be patient** — Not everyone is ready now; the best outreach builds relationships over time
