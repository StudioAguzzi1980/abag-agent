## Key Team Member Profile Optimization

### Headline Formula
`[Title] at ABAG | Helping U.S. Companies Succeed in Italy | [Specialty Area]`

**Example:**
> `Managing Director at ABAG | Helping American Businesses Enter Italy & Europe | Company Formation & Tax Advisory`

### About Section Structure

1. **Hook** — Open with a compelling statement about who you help and how
2. **What you do** — Describe your role and ABAG's services
3. **Your expertise** — Highlight specific areas of specialization
4. **Social proof** — Reference clients or results (without specifics)
5. **CTA** — Invite people to connect or schedule a conversation

**Example About Section:**
```
Every week, I speak with American entrepreneurs and corporate development executives 
who are excited about Italy — but frustrated by how complicated it seems to get started.

My job at ABAG is to make that process simple, clear, and reliable.

I specialize in [area] for U.S. companies entering Italy — covering [specific services]. 
Over the past [X] years, I've helped companies across [industries] establish and grow 
their Italian operations.

If you're exploring Italy or Europe as a market, I'm happy to talk. Send me a message 
or connect — I respond to every serious inquiry.
```

## Profile Checklist

- [ ] Professional headshot (not a logo or casual photo)
- [ ] Cover image: ABAG-branded or relevant professional image
- [ ] Headline: includes role, ABAG, and value proposition keyword
- [ ] About section: complete and keyword-rich
- [ ] Experience: ABAG role fully described; previous relevant roles listed
- [ ] Skills: 10+ skills listed, with top 3 endorsed
- [ ] Education and certifications: complete
- [ ] Featured section: link to ABAG website, key article, or guide
- [ ] Contact info: email and website visible to connections
- [ ] Location: accurate (Italy or relevant city)
- [ ] Recommendations: 3+ from colleagues or clients
- [ ] Connection count: work toward 500+ for credibility

## LinkedIn Company Page Content — Best Practices

- Post 3–5 times per week (see `/linkedin/content_calendar.md`)
- Always include a visual (image, carousel, or video)
- Use 3–5 hashtags per post: mix popular (#Italy, #Business) with niche (#ItalianTax, #UScompanies)
- Tag relevant LinkedIn newsletters, events, or profiles where appropriate
- Respond to all comments within 24 hours
- Track analytics: focus on impressions, follower growth, and post engagement rate
