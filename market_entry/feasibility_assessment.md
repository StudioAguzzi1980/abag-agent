# Feasibility Assessment Framework

## Purpose

Before committing to Italian market entry, every U.S. company should conduct a structured feasibility assessment. This document provides a framework for evaluating whether the Italian market represents a viable and attractive opportunity for a specific business.

ABAG conducts feasibility assessments as a standalone engagement or as the first phase of a broader Market Entry Strategy project.

---

## Feasibility Assessment — Key Dimensions

### Dimension 1 — Market Demand

**Question:** Is there genuine demand in Italy for what you offer?

**Assessment activities:**
- Review of Italian market size data for your product/service category
- Consumer and B2B buyer research (desk research and, where appropriate, primary interviews)
- Analysis of import/export trends for your sector
- Review of Italian consumer attitudes and purchasing behavior
- Identification of existing Italian and international competitors serving the same need

**Key output:** Market size estimate, demand evidence, competitive density

---

### Dimension 2 — Regulatory Environment

**Question:** Can your product or service be legally sold in Italy, and at what compliance cost?

**Assessment activities:**
- Review of EU and Italian regulatory requirements for your product/service category
- Identification of required certifications, licenses, or permits
- Assessment of labeling and packaging requirements (especially for food, beverages, consumer goods)
- Review of professional authorization requirements (for service businesses)
- GDPR and data protection implications

**Key output:** Regulatory clearance assessment, compliance cost estimate, required pre-market actions

---

### Dimension 3 — Business Model Viability

**Question:** Can your U.S. business model work in Italy as-is, or does it require significant adaptation?

**Assessment activities:**
- Pricing analysis — Are Italian customers willing to pay at your required price point?
- Value proposition fit — Does your differentiation resonate in the Italian market context?
- Product/service adaptation — What changes are needed for Italian customers?
- Brand recognition — Is your brand known or unknown in Italy?
- Operational model — Can your supply chain, service delivery, or technology stack work in Italy?

**Key output:** Business model adaptation requirements, localization investment estimate

---

### Dimension 4 — Competition and Positioning

**Question:** Is the Italian market already saturated, or is there a gap your business can fill?

**Assessment activities:**
- Competitive landscape mapping (local Italian competitors, EU competitors, other U.S. entrants)
- Competitor positioning, pricing, and channel analysis
- Identification of underserved niches or customer segments
- Assessment of barriers to competitive response

**Key output:** Competitive landscape summary, positioning opportunity

---

### Dimension 5 — Financial Viability

**Question:** Does the Italian market opportunity justify the investment required?

**Assessment activities:**
- Revenue projection for Year 1–3 based on market assessment
- Cost estimate for market entry: company formation, banking, staffing, office, marketing
- Operating cost model: accounting, compliance, logistics, etc.
- Breakeven analysis
- Return on investment projection
- Comparison to alternative market opportunities

**Key output:** P&L projection, breakeven timeline, investment required

---

### Dimension 6 — Organizational Readiness

**Question:** Does your company have the capacity to execute an Italian market entry?

**Assessment activities:**
- Management bandwidth — who will own the Italian market?
- Language and cultural competence in the team
- International experience of leadership
- Capital availability for the investment phase
- Technology and operations scalability

**Key output:** Organizational readiness score, capability gaps to address

## Feasibility Assessment Scoring

Use this scoring matrix to assess overall feasibility:

| Dimension | Score 1–5 | Weight | Weighted Score |

| Competition and Positioning | | 15% | |
| Financial Viability | | 15% | |
| Organizational Readiness | | 10% | |
| **Total** | | 100% | |

**Scoring guide:**
- 1 = Very unfavorable / high risk
- 3 = Neutral / moderate opportunity
- 5 = Very favorable / strong opportunity

**Interpretation:**
- Weighted total ≥ 3.5 = Strong case for entry; proceed with full market entry planning
- Weighted total 2.5–3.5 = Conditional entry; address specific weaknesses first
- Weighted total < 2.5 = Significant concerns; reconsider timing or approach
