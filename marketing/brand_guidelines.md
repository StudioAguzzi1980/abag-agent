# ABAG Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

### Mission Statement

> "ABAG empowers American businesses to establish and grow in Italy and Europe — combining deep local expertise with a genuine understanding of U.S. business culture."

### Brand Positioning

ABAG is positioned as the **trusted transatlantic bridge** for American companies entering Italy. We occupy a distinct space between:
- Pure Italian advisory firms (strong local knowledge, limited U.S. context)
- U.S. international law and accounting firms (U.S. expertise, limited Italy-specific depth)

ABAG's competitive advantage is the intersection of both — bilingual, bicultural, and fully capable on both sides of the Atlantic.

## Brand Voice

### Tone of Voice

- **Professional** — We communicate as trusted advisors, not salespeople
- **Clear and direct** — No jargon without explanation; no unnecessary complexity
- **Confident** — We know our field; we speak with authority and precision
- **Approachable** — We are accessible and responsive; we don't condescend
- **Warm but not casual** — We are friendly and human, but always professional

### What ABAG Sounds Like

✅ **Do say:**
- "Here's what you need to know about Italian company formation..."
- "The most important factor in your decision is..."
- "We recommend scheduling a consultation to explore your specific situation."

❌ **Don't say:**
- "It's really complicated, but basically..." (undermines confidence)
- "You absolutely must do X or you'll be in serious trouble." (alarmist)
- "No problem!" / "Awesome!" (too casual for the advisory context)

## Visual Identity

### Logo Usage

- The ABAG logo should always appear on a white or light background
- Minimum size: 100px width in digital; 1 inch in print
- Do not stretch, rotate, or alter the logo in any way
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the logo's letterforms on all sides

### Color Palette

| Color | Use | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| **ABAG Navy** | Primary brand color; headings, key accents | `#1B2A4A` |
| **ABAG Gold** | Accent; highlights, CTA buttons | `#C9A84C` |
| **White** | Backgrounds, text on dark | `#FFFFFF` |
| **Light Gray** | Secondary backgrounds, dividers | `#F5F5F5` |
| **Dark Gray** | Body text | `#333333` |

### Typography

- **Headings:** [Brand-approved heading font — e.g., Playfair Display or Libre Baskerville]
- **Body text:** [Brand-approved body font — e.g., Source Sans Pro or Open Sans]
- **Minimum body text size:** 14px digital / 10pt print

---

## Photography and Imagery Guidelines

- Use imagery that reflects European professional environments: boardrooms, city skylines, handshakes, modern offices
- Avoid generic stock photos with obviously American settings
- Prefer imagery featuring diverse, professional subjects
- Italian landmarks may be used sparingly; avoid clichés (Colosseum, pizza, gondolas)
- All images must be licensed for commercial use

---

## Messaging Hierarchy

1. **Primary message:** ABAG helps American businesses succeed in Italy and Europe
2. **Supporting message:** We handle the regulatory, fiscal, and administrative complexity
3. **Proof:** Track record across multiple industries; bilingual team; local relationships
