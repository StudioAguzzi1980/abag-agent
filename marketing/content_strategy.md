# ABAG Content Strategy

## Purpose

This document outlines ABAG's content strategy — what content to create, for whom, on which channels, and with what goals — to build brand awareness, generate leads, and establish ABAG as the authoritative resource for U.S. companies entering Italy and Europe.

---

## Content Marketing Goals

1. **Awareness** — Ensure ABAG is known to U.S. entrepreneurs and corporate development teams considering Italy
2. **Authority** — Establish ABAG as the trusted expert on doing business in Italy for U.S. companies
3. **Lead generation** — Convert content consumers into prospects via CTAs, gated resources, and consultations
4. **Client retention** — Keep existing clients informed and engaged with relevant updates

---

## Core Content Pillars

All content should fall within one of the following five pillars:

| Pillar | Topics | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Italian Business 101** | Company formation, entity types, Italian bureaucracy | Awareness and education for early-stage prospects |
| **Tax & Compliance** | Italian tax system, IRES/IRAP/VAT, U.S.–Italy treaty, compliance calendar | Authority building; reach corporate and investor personas |
| **Market Entry** | Italy market insights, sector deep dives, go-to-market strategies | Engage growth-oriented entrepreneurs and corporate development |
| **Banking & Finance** | Opening accounts, banking tips, payment solutions | Practical, high-demand content; strong engagement driver |
| **ABAG Expertise** | Case studies, team expertise, client successes, industry news | Social proof and trust building |

---

## Content Types

### Pillar Content (Flagship)
Long-form guides, e-books, and comprehensive reference documents. Updated quarterly.

**Examples:**
- *The Complete Guide to Company Formation in Italy for U.S. Entrepreneurs*
- *Italian Tax Guide for American Businesses 2024/2025*
- *How to Open a Corporate Bank Account in Italy*

**Distribution:** Website, downloadable PDF, gated lead magnet

---

### Blog Articles
800–1,500 word articles targeting specific questions and search intent.

**Examples:**
- "SRL vs. SPA: Which Italian Company Structure is Right for You?"
- "5 Things U.S. Companies Must Know Before Entering the Italian Market"
- "How the U.S.–Italy Tax Treaty Affects Your Italian Subsidiary"

**Cadence:** 2–4 per month
**Distribution:** Website blog, LinkedIn articles, email newsletter

---

### LinkedIn Content
Short-form posts, native articles, and carousel content for professional audience.

**Examples:**
- "3 mistakes American companies make when incorporating in Italy (and how to avoid them)"
- Carousel: "Italian company formation — step by step"
- Quick tip: "Did you know: Italy's VAT rate is 22%, but reduced rates of 10%, 5%, and 4% apply to specific categories."

**Cadence:** 3–5 posts per week
**See:** `/linkedin/content_calendar.md`

---

### Case Studies
Narrative accounts of client engagements (anonymized or with permission).

**Examples:**
- "How a New York Tech Startup Incorporated in Milan in 6 Weeks"
- "From U.S. Market to Italian Distribution: A Food Brand's Journey"

**Cadence:** 1–2 per quarter
**Distribution:** Website, LinkedIn, sales presentations

---

### Webinars / Events
Live or recorded online sessions on high-interest topics.

**Examples:**
- "Entering the Italian Market in 2025: What U.S. Companies Need to Know"
- "Italian Tax Obligations for American Businesses — Live Q&A"

**Cadence:** Monthly or quarterly
**Distribution:** LinkedIn events, email list, partnerships with Italian chambers of commerce and trade associations

---

### Email Newsletter
Monthly digest of regulatory updates, market insights, and ABAG content.

**Audience:** Existing clients, opted-in prospects
**Cadence:** Monthly
**See:** `/email_templates/monthly_newsletter.md`
