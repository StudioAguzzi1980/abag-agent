# ABAG Messaging Framework

## Purpose

This framework provides a structured approach to communicating ABAG's value across all channels. Use it as the foundation for website copy, LinkedIn content, email campaigns, presentations, and sales conversations.

---

## Core Value Proposition

**Primary statement:**
> "ABAG is the specialist advisory firm for American companies entering Italy and Europe — combining deep local expertise with a genuine understanding of U.S. business culture."

**Elevator pitch (30 seconds):**
> "ABAG helps U.S. companies enter and operate in Italy without the complexity and risk that typically comes with cross-border expansion. We handle everything from company incorporation and tax planning to banking setup and ongoing compliance — so our clients can focus on building their business, not navigating Italian bureaucracy."

---

## Audience-Specific Messaging

### For U.S. Entrepreneurs / Founders

**Primary pain points:**
- Fear of the unknown in a foreign market
- Concern about regulatory complexity
- Distrust of Italian bureaucracy
- Uncertainty about timelines and costs

**ABAG message:**
> "Starting a business in Italy doesn't have to be overwhelming. ABAG handles the paperwork, the authorities, and the complexity — so you can focus on what you do best. We've done this for dozens of American entrepreneurs and we'll do it for you."

**Key proof points:**
- Streamlined, managed process
- No need to travel to Italy in most cases
- Fixed-fee clarity
- English-first communication

---

### For Corporate Development / U.S. Multinationals

**Primary pain points:**
- Board-level accountability for compliance and risk
- Need for reliable local infrastructure fast
- U.S. GAAP reporting requirements
- Integration with HQ financial systems

**ABAG message:**
> "ABAG gives U.S. multinationals a reliable, compliant Italian operation — fast. We set up the entity, manage the local accounting and compliance, and provide U.S.-GAAP-aligned reporting. One firm. No gaps."

**Key proof points:**
- Experience with U.S. parent company reporting structures
- Compliance track record
- Scalable service model
- Integration with U.S. finance teams

---

### For Investors (PE / Family Office)

**Primary pain points:**
- Italian portfolio company governance concerns
- Regulatory compliance risk in acquired businesses
- Need for local advisor to monitor Italian entity

**ABAG message:**
> "ABAG supports U.S. investors with Italian portfolio companies — providing compliance monitoring, financial oversight, and advisory services that give you confidence in your Italian investment."
