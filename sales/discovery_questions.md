# Discovery Questions — Client Intake Guide

## Purpose

Use these questions during the initial discovery call or meeting with a prospective client. The goal is to fully understand the client's situation, objectives, constraints, and timeline — and to identify which ABAG services are most relevant.

Take notes in the CRM during or immediately after the call.

---

## Section 1 — Company and Business Background

1. Can you give me a brief overview of your business — what you do, where you operate, and your current stage?
2. What industry are you in, and how would you describe your target customers?
3. How many employees do you currently have? Where are they based?
4. What is your approximate annual revenue (or funding stage if a startup)?
5. Who is the primary owner or decision-maker for this initiative?
6. Is there a U.S. parent company, holding company, or investor involved? Who are the key stakeholders?

---

## Section 2 — Italy / Europe Objectives

7. What is driving your decision to expand into Italy (or Europe)?
8. What does success look like for you in Italy in the first 12–24 months?
9. Do you already have any presence, customers, or partners in Italy or Europe?
10. Are you looking to serve Italian customers, use Italy as a base for EU operations, or both?
11. Do you have a preferred Italian region (e.g., Milan, Rome, Turin, Naples) or are you open to guidance?

---

## Section 3 — Legal Structure and Formation

12. Have you already considered what type of legal entity you want to establish? (S.r.l., branch, other)
13. Who will be the directors of the Italian entity? Are they U.S. citizens?
14. Who will be the shareholders? Are there multiple shareholders or is it a single parent company?
15. Do you anticipate hiring employees in Italy? How many, and in what roles?
16. Will the Italian entity enter into contracts, hold assets, or generate revenue? Or is it more of a presence for coordination?

---

## Section 4 — Tax and Financial Structure

17. Is the Italian entity intended to be a subsidiary of your U.S. company, or a standalone Italian entity?
18. Have you already thought about how profits will flow between Italy and the U.S.?
19. Are there any intercompany transactions you anticipate (management fees, royalties, loans)?
20. Do you have a U.S. CPA or tax advisor already? Would they need to coordinate with ABAG?
21. What are your financial reporting requirements — do you need Italian GAAP, U.S. GAAP reconciliation, or IFRS?

---

## Section 5 — Banking and Payments

22. Do you have any existing relationships with banks that have Italian operations (Citi, JP Morgan, UniCredit, etc.)?
23. What volume and types of transactions do you anticipate (invoices to Italian customers, salaries, intercompany transfers)?
24. Do you need multi-currency capability, or will you primarily transact in euros?
25. Have you considered fintech or payment service providers as part of your banking solution?

---

## Section 6 — Timeline and Urgency

26. What is your target date to be operational in Italy?
27. Is this timeline driven by any external factors (contracts, investor commitments, regulatory deadlines)?
28. What is the biggest risk or concern you have about this process?
29. Have you started any part of this process already (e.g., spoken to a lawyer, started a company registration)?

---

## Section 7 — Budget and Decision Process

30. Do you have a budget set aside for professional advisory services for this project?
31. Are you evaluating other advisors or firms alongside ABAG?
32. Who needs to approve the engagement on your side?
33. What would a decision timeline look like for you?

---

## Post-Call Actions

- [ ] Log all responses in CRM
- [ ] Identify which ABAG services are relevant
- [ ] Flag any red flags (budget concerns, competitor involvement, unclear ownership)
- [ ] Send a follow-up email summarizing key points within 24 hours
- [ ] Prepare a tailored proposal within 5 business days
- [ ] Schedule the next touchpoint before ending the call

---

*These questions are a guide, not a script. Adapt based on the flow of conversation. The goal is to listen actively and uncover what the client truly needs.*
