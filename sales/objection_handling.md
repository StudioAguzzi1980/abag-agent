# Objection Handling Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides ABAG sales professionals with structured responses to the most common objections encountered during the sales process. Each entry includes the objection, the underlying concern it reveals, and a recommended response strategy.

---

## Objection 1: "It's too expensive."

**Underlying concern:** The prospect is uncertain about the value or return on investment.

**Response:**

> "I completely understand — professional services are a meaningful investment, and it's right to be thoughtful about it. What I'd encourage you to consider is the cost of getting it wrong. Errors in Italian company formation, tax structuring, or compliance can result in penalties, double taxation, or significant delays that cost far more than the advisory fee. ABAG's role is to prevent those problems from arising in the first place.
>
> That said, I'd like to understand your budget so we can explore whether there's a scope that works for both parties. What level of investment feels realistic for this phase of the project?"

**Key principle:** Reframe cost as risk mitigation. Make the comparison to the cost of errors, not to other advisory firms.

---

## Objection 2: "We'll use a local Italian firm."

**Underlying concern:** The prospect believes a local Italian advisor will be cheaper or more knowledgeable.

**Response:**

> "That's a fair consideration — and there are excellent Italian professionals we work alongside. The key question is whether they have experience working with U.S. businesses specifically. American companies have unique needs: U.S. parent company reporting, cross-border tax treaty compliance, GAAP reconciliation, and familiarity with how U.S. decision-makers operate. Many local Italian firms are excellent within Italian law but lack that transatlantic context.
>
> ABAG fills that gap — we combine deep Italian expertise with genuine fluency in U.S. business culture. Many of our clients have come to us after an initial experience with a purely local firm that didn't work out."

**Key principle:** Acknowledge the validity, then differentiate on the bilingual/bicultural advantage.

---

## Objection 3: "We're not ready yet."

**Underlying concern:** The prospect is in an early stage and may be concerned about committing resources before plans are firm.

**Response:**

> "That makes complete sense — and there's no pressure to move before you're ready. One thing I'd suggest is that the earlier we're involved, the more value we can add. Choices made at the planning stage — around legal structure, tax planning, and banking — have significant long-term implications. It's much easier (and cheaper) to get these right from the start than to restructure later.
>
> Would it make sense to start with an initial consultation or a market assessment? That way, you're investing in clarity and preparation — with no obligation to proceed further until you're ready."

**Key principle:** Lower the commitment threshold. Offer a small, easy first step.

---

## Objection 4: "We're evaluating other options."

**Underlying concern:** The prospect is conducting a competitive process and wants to be sure they're making the right choice.

**Response:**

> "Of course — that's a wise approach for any significant professional services engagement. May I ask what the main criteria are for your evaluation? And are you comparing ABAG to other specialist advisors, or more to the option of managing parts of this internally?
>
> I'd like to make sure you have everything you need from us to make the right decision. I'm happy to provide references from similar clients, or to walk through our approach in more detail for any specific service area."

**Key principle:** Treat as an opportunity to understand the competitive landscape and reinforce ABAG's differentiators.

---

## Objection 5: "We already have a law firm / accounting firm handling this."

**Underlying concern:** The prospect believes their existing advisors can handle the Italian setup.

**Response:**

> "Great — having a trusted legal and accounting relationship is valuable. The question is whether they have dedicated Italy/EU capability. Italian company formation, VAT compliance, and Italian GAAP accounting require very specific local knowledge and relationships. ABAG can work seamlessly alongside your existing advisors as the Italy-specialist layer — many of our clients have a U.S. CPA or law firm for their domestic matters and rely on ABAG exclusively for the Italian side."

**Key principle:** Position ABAG as a specialist complement, not a replacement.

---

## Objection 6: "How do I know ABAG will deliver?"

**Underlying concern:** The prospect is unsure about quality, reliability, or track record.

**Response:**

> "That's a fair and important question. I'd suggest three things: first, I can connect you with clients who've been through a similar process with ABAG — they're the best judges of our quality. Second, I can walk you through exactly how we manage a project like yours — the steps, the communication, and how we handle issues when they arise. Third, our engagement letters clearly define deliverables, timelines, and responsibilities so there are no surprises.
>
> Would references from current clients be helpful at this stage?"

**Key principle:** Provide social proof, process transparency, and contractual commitment.

---

## Objection 7: "Can you just help with one thing for now?"

**Underlying concern:** The prospect wants to limit scope and cost initially.

**Response:**

> "Absolutely — we're happy to start wherever makes the most sense for you. Many clients engage us initially for a specific project, like company formation or tax advice, and expand the relationship as they see the value. Just be aware that some areas are interconnected — for example, company formation decisions affect tax structure, and tax structure affects banking options. We'll flag any dependencies so you can make an informed decision about scope.
>
> What aspect would you like to start with?"

**Key principle:** Accept the limited engagement, start building trust, and flag natural upsell points organically.
