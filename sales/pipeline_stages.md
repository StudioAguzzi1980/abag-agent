# Sales Pipeline Stages — Reference Guide

## Overview

This document defines each stage in the ABAG sales pipeline, the criteria for advancement, the required actions at each stage, and the expected conversion rates.

---

## Stage 1 — Lead

**Definition:** An individual or company has expressed initial interest in ABAG's services, or has been identified as a target prospect.

**Sources:**
- Website inquiry or contact form
- LinkedIn message or connection
- Referral from existing client or partner
- Email campaign response
- Conference or networking event contact
- Cold outreach response

**Entry criteria:** Contact details captured and interest noted.

**Required actions:**
- Log contact in CRM within 24 hours
- Assign to a sales owner
- Initial response (phone call or email) within 24 hours
- Qualify for advancement to Stage 2

**Exit criteria:** Qualifying criteria confirmed via initial conversation.

**Expected conversion to Stage 2:** 50–70%

---

## Stage 2 — Qualified

**Definition:** Discovery call completed. The prospect has a genuine need, is the right type of client for ABAG, and has indicated intent to proceed.

**Qualification criteria (BANT):**
- **Budget:** Has or is willing to allocate a budget for advisory services
- **Authority:** Is a decision-maker or has confirmed access to decision-makers
- **Need:** Has a specific, defined need that ABAG can address
- **Timeline:** Has a realistic and reasonably near-term timeline

**Required actions:**
- Log full discovery notes in CRM
- Identify relevant ABAG services
- Confirm next steps with prospect
- Prepare proposal within 5 business days

**Exit criteria:** Proposal sent to prospect.

**Expected conversion to Stage 3:** 75–85%

---

## Stage 3 — Proposal Sent

**Definition:** A formal, written proposal has been delivered to the prospect.

**Required actions:**
- Confirm receipt with the prospect within 1 business day of sending
- Schedule a proposal review call within 3 business days
- Follow up if no response within 5 business days
- Address any questions about scope, fees, or timeline

**Exit criteria:** Prospect has indicated intent to proceed, requested revision, or engaged in commercial negotiation.

**Expected conversion to Stage 4 or 5:** 60–75%

---

## Stage 4 — Negotiation

**Definition:** The prospect is reviewing or negotiating the proposal — scope, fees, or terms are under active discussion.

**Required actions:**
- Document all negotiation points in CRM
- Assess whether concessions are warranted (involve management for significant discounts)
- Prepare revised proposal if scope changes are agreed
- Maintain momentum — do not allow negotiations to stall
- Set a decision deadline with the prospect

**Exit criteria:** Agreement reached (advance to Closed Won) or negotiations end without agreement (advance to Closed Lost).

**Expected conversion to Closed Won:** 60–80%

---

## Stage 5 — Closed Won

**Definition:** The engagement letter has been signed and the initial payment has been received.

**Required actions:**
- Mark as Closed Won in CRM immediately
- Update revenue forecast
- Notify the ABAG delivery team within 1 business day
- Introduce the client to their ABAG project manager
- Initiate the client onboarding process (see `/client_onboarding/overview.md`)
- Send a welcome email (see `/email_templates/onboarding_welcome.md`)

---

## Stage 6 — Closed Lost

**Definition:** The prospect has chosen not to proceed at this time.

**Required actions:**
- Log the reason for loss in CRM (mandatory)
- Send a professional, gracious close-out email
- Set a re-engagement reminder (typically 6 months)
- Analyze patterns in loss reasons quarterly to improve sales process

**Common loss reasons to track:**
- Price / budget not aligned
- Chose a competing firm
- Decided not to expand to Italy
- Timeline pushed out significantly
- No response / ghosted after proposal

## Pipeline Metrics — Targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Monthly new leads | [Set by management] |
| Lead to Qualified | ≥ 60% |
| Qualified to Proposal | ≥ 80% |
| Proposal to Close | ≥ 40% |
| Average sales cycle | 30–60 days |
| Average deal value (ACV) | ≥ €10,000 |
| Pipeline coverage ratio | 3x monthly target |

---

## CRM Hygiene Rules

- No opportunity should remain in Stage 1 for more than 5 business days
- No opportunity should remain in Stage 3 (Proposal Sent) for more than 20 business days without an update
- All deal fields must be completed: expected close date, deal value, lead source, service lines
- Loss reasons must be logged within 24 hours of any lost deal

---

*Pipeline stage definitions should be reviewed and updated quarterly to reflect the current sales reality.*
