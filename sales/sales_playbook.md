# ABAG Sales Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook provides ABAG sales professionals and advisors with a repeatable framework for engaging prospective clients, qualifying opportunities, presenting value, and converting inquiries into signed engagements.

---

## Target Client Profile

### Primary Personas

| Persona | Description |
|---|---|
| **U.S. Entrepreneur** | American founder or business owner considering entry into Italy or Europe |
| **U.S. Corporate Development** | VP or Director at a U.S. mid-cap or large-cap company exploring Italian subsidiary or acquisition |
| **Returning Expatriate** | American or dual citizen returning to Italy and starting or acquiring a business |
| **European-American Dual Business** | Company already operating in Italy seeking to professionalize advisory relationships |
| **U.S. Investor** | Private equity or family office investing in Italian businesses or real estate |

### Ideal Client Characteristics

- U.S.-based individual or company
- Revenue of $500K+ (for established businesses) or well-funded startup
- Clear business objective in Italy (not purely exploratory)
- Decision-maker is engaged or accessible
- Willing to invest in professional services to reduce risk and accelerate entry

---

## Sales Stages

| Stage | Definition | Key Action |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Lead** | Initial contact or inquiry received | Log in CRM, qualify within 24 hours |
| **2. Qualified** | Discovery call completed, fit confirmed | Document needs, advance to proposal |
| **3. Proposal** | Proposal sent and under review | Follow up within 5 business days |
| **4. Negotiation** | Scope or pricing discussion in progress | Address objections, revise if needed |
| **5. Closed Won** | Engagement letter signed | Initiate onboarding, notify delivery team |
| **6. Closed Lost** | Prospect chose not to proceed | Log reason, set re-engagement reminder |

- **End-to-end capability** — One firm covers company formation, tax, accounting, compliance, and banking
- **Speed and reliability** — Established relationships with Italian authorities and banking partners accelerate every process
- **Track record** — Demonstrated success across technology, manufacturing, F&B, and professional services sectors

## Qualifying Questions (BANT Framework)

### Budget
- "Do you have a budget allocated for professional advisory services in Italy?"
- "What level of investment are you comfortable with for the initial setup phase?"

### Authority
- "Are you the decision-maker for this engagement, or are there other stakeholders involved?"
- "Who else in your organization needs to be part of this conversation?"

### Need
- "What is driving the decision to enter Italy now?"
- "What do you see as the biggest challenge or risk in this process?"
- "Have you attempted this before? What happened?"

### Timeline
- "When are you hoping to be operational in Italy?"
- "Are there any external deadlines (board decisions, client commitments, investor requirements) driving the timeline?"

## Objection Handling

See `/sales/objection_handling.md` for a full objection response guide.

**Quick reference:**

| Objection | Response Summary |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive." | Reframe as risk mitigation; cost of errors far exceeds advisory fees |
| "We'll use a local Italian firm." | Highlight ABAG's bilingual/bicultural advantage; local firms often lack U.S. context |
| "We're not ready yet." | Offer a lower-commitment starting point (consultation, market assessment) |
| "We're evaluating other options." | Understand the alternatives; reinforce ABAG's differentiated value |

## Proposal Best Practices

1. **Personalize** — Reference the client's specific business and objectives
2. **Scope clearly** — Define exactly what is included and what is not
3. **Price transparently** — Break down fees by service component
4. **Set expectations** — Include realistic timelines and client responsibilities
5. **Include social proof** — Reference relevant client experiences (with permission)
6. **Propose next steps** — Always close the proposal with a clear call to action

See `/sales/proposal_template.md` for the standard proposal template.

## CRM and Pipeline Management

- All leads must be entered in CRM within 24 hours of first contact
- Stage updates required within 24 hours of any client interaction
- Pipeline review: weekly with sales team
- Stale opportunities (no movement in 30 days): review and escalate or close
