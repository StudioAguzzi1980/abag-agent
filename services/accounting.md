# Service: Accounting

## Service Overview

ABAG provides full-scope accounting and financial management services for U.S.-owned businesses operating in Italy. Our accounting team ensures compliance with Italian statutory requirements while delivering the financial transparency that U.S. parent companies and investors require.

---

## Core Accounting Services

### Bookkeeping

- Transaction recording in compliance with Italian accounting standards (OIC — Organismo Italiano di Contabilità)
- Maintenance of mandatory Italian accounting registers (libro giornale, libro degli inventari)
- Accounts payable and accounts receivable management
- Bank reconciliations
- Fixed asset register management and depreciation schedules
- Management of electronic invoicing (fatturazione elettronica via Sistema di Interscambio)

### Statutory Annual Accounts

- Preparation of the annual financial statements (bilancio d'esercizio) in accordance with Italian law
  - Balance sheet (stato patrimoniale)
  - Income statement (conto economico)
  - Notes to the accounts (nota integrativa)
  - Directors' report (relazione sulla gestione), where required
- Coordination with statutory auditors or the Board of Statutory Auditors (Collegio Sindacale) where applicable
- Filing with the Chamber of Commerce within legal deadlines

### Management Accounts

- Monthly and quarterly management accounts in English for U.S. parent company reporting
- Budget vs. actual variance analysis
- Cash flow forecasting and reporting
- KPI dashboards tailored to client requirements
- GAAP reconciliation (Italian GAAP to U.S. GAAP or IFRS) where required

### VAT Accounting

- Monthly or quarterly VAT return preparation and filing (liquidazione IVA)
- Annual VAT summary return (dichiarazione IVA)
- Intrastat declarations for intra-EU trade
- Electronic invoicing compliance monitoring

### Payroll Accounting

- Monthly payroll calculations in compliance with Italian labor law and applicable collective bargaining agreements (CCNL)
- Preparation and filing of monthly payroll tax returns (modello F24)
- Social security contributions (INPS) and workplace injury insurance (INAIL) compliance
- Annual 770 return filing (employer tax return)
- CUD / CU preparation for employees
- Employee expense reimbursement management

## Italian Accounting Standards

Italian companies are required to prepare statutory accounts under Italian GAAP (OIC). Key principles include:

- **Prudence** — Assets and liabilities recognized conservatively
- **Going concern** — Accounts prepared on the assumption of continued operations
- **Consistency** — Accounting policies applied consistently year on year
- **Accruals basis** — Income and expenses recognized when earned/incurred

S.p.A. and S.r.l. companies above certain thresholds (assets > €4.4 million, revenue > €8.8 million, or employees > 50) are required to appoint statutory auditors.
