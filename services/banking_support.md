# Service: Banking Support

## Service Overview

Opening a corporate bank account in Italy is consistently cited as one of the most challenging steps for foreign-owned businesses. Italian banks are subject to strict KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) requirements, and their processes can be opaque and time-consuming for international clients.

ABAG removes these barriers by preparing documentation, selecting the right banking partner, and facilitating introductions and relationships with banking institutions on the client's behalf.

---

## Key Challenges for Foreign Businesses

- Italian banks typically require extensive documentation for foreign-owned entities
- Beneficial ownership verification for non-EU parent companies can be complex
- Many Italian banks prefer or require in-person meetings, creating logistical challenges for U.S.-based clients
- Internal bank compliance processes can create significant delays without specialist guidance
- Language barriers add friction to the documentation and communication process

## Requirements Before Account Opening

Most Italian banks require the following to be in place before opening a corporate account:

1. **Complete company registration** — The company must be fully registered in the Italian Business Register
2. **Registered office** — A valid Italian registered address (ABAG can provide this)
3. **Italian tax code (codice fiscale)** — For the company and all key individuals
4. **VAT number (Partita IVA)** — Obtained from the Agenzia delle Entrate
5. **Director and shareholder identification** — Passports and proof of address for all beneficial owners
