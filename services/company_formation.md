# Service: Company Formation in Italy

## Service Overview

ABAG guides U.S. clients through the entire process of establishing a legal business entity in Italy. This includes selecting the appropriate corporate structure, preparing and coordinating all required documentation, liaising with Italian notaries and government authorities, and supporting post-incorporation setup.

---

## Available Entity Types

### S.r.l. — Società a Responsabilità Limitata (Limited Liability Company)

The most common structure for foreign-owned businesses in Italy.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Minimum share capital | €1 (simplified) or €10,000 (standard) |
| Liability | Limited to share capital contribution |
| Governance | One or more directors (amministratori); no mandatory board |
| Shareholders | Minimum 1; can be individuals or legal entities |
| Best for | SMEs, subsidiaries, holding companies, startups |

### S.p.A. — Società per Azioni (Joint-Stock Company)

Suitable for larger enterprises and companies anticipating external investment.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Minimum share capital | €50,000 (at least 25% paid in at incorporation) |
| Liability | Limited to share capital contribution |
| Governance | Board of Directors required; statutory auditors required above certain thresholds |
| Shareholders | Minimum 1; shares are freely transferable |
| Best for | Large subsidiaries, companies seeking public listing or external funding |

### Branch Office (Sede Secondaria)

An extension of the foreign parent company operating in Italy. Not a separate legal entity.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Legal personality | None — parent company bears full liability |
| Share capital | Not required |
| Governance | Appointed local representative (preposto) |
| Best for | Companies that want an Italian presence without a separate subsidiary |
| Tax note | Branch profits are subject to Italian taxation |

### Representative Office (Ufficio di Rappresentanza)

A non-commercial Italian presence for market research, promotion, and liaison activities.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Revenue-generating | No — cannot invoice or conduct commercial activities |
| Registration | Required with the Chamber of Commerce |
| Best for | Market research, pre-launch exploration, trade liaison |
| Tax note | Generally not subject to IRES if correctly structured |

## Incorporation Process — S.r.l.

1. **Pre-incorporation planning** — Determine structure, share capital, directors, and shareholders
2. **Obtain Italian tax codes (codice fiscale)** — Required for all directors, shareholders, and beneficial owners
3. **Prepare incorporation documents** — Articles of incorporation (atto costitutivo) and company bylaws (statuto)
4. **Notarial deed** — Executed before an Italian notary (notaio)
5. **Register with the Chamber of Commerce** — Via the Italian Business Register (Registro delle Imprese)
6. **Register for VAT** — Obtain Partita IVA from the Agenzia delle Entrate
7. **Open corporate bank account** — Required to deposit share capital (for standard S.r.l.)
8. **Register for INPS** — If the company will employ staff

**Typical timeline:** 3–6 weeks from completion of all required documentation

---

## Required Documentation (from U.S. clients)

- Valid passport for all directors and shareholders
- Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement, less than 3 months old)
- U.S. company documents (if a corporate shareholder): Certificate of Incorporation, Articles, Certificate of Good Standing — apostilled and officially translated into Italian
- Power of attorney (if ABAG is authorized to act on the client's behalf)

---

## Post-Incorporation Actions

After registration, ABAG assists with:

- Opening the corporate bank account
- Registering for social security (INPS) and health insurance (INAIL) if hiring employees
- Setting up accounting and bookkeeping systems
- Registering for applicable sector licenses or permits
- Establishing the registered office (sede legale)

---

## ABAG's Role

ABAG acts as the client's representative and coordinator throughout the entire process:

- Prepares all documentation
- Coordinates with the Italian notary
- Liaises with the Chamber of Commerce, Agenzia delle Entrate, and other authorities
- Provides a registered office address if required
- Manages the full process remotely — the client does not need to travel to Italy for most incorporations
