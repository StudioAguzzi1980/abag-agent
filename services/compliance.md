# Service: Corporate Compliance

## Service Overview

Operating a legal entity in Italy involves a continuous stream of regulatory, governance, and filing obligations. ABAG's compliance service ensures that your Italian company meets all mandatory requirements — so you can focus on running your business without worrying about penalties, missed deadlines, or regulatory exposure.

---

## Areas of Compliance Coverage

### Business Register Obligations

- Annual filing of financial statements (bilancio) with the Chamber of Commerce
- Registration of changes to company data: directors, shareholders, registered office, share capital, bylaws
- Maintenance of the company's Business Register (Visura Camerale) profile
- Filing of beneficial ownership declarations (Titolare Effettivo) under Italian AML law

### Corporate Governance

- Organization of annual shareholders' meetings (assemblea ordinaria) to approve accounts
- Preparation of board resolutions and shareholders' resolutions in Italian
- Maintenance of the corporate minute book (libro dei verbali)
- Advice on governance obligations specific to S.r.l. and S.p.A. companies
- Director appointment and resignation filings

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance

- Italian AML obligations under D.Lgs. 231/2007 (implementing EU AML directives)
- Customer due diligence (CDD) procedures
- Suspicious transaction reporting protocols
- Beneficial ownership documentation
- Periodic AML risk assessments

### Corporate Liability (D.Lgs. 231/2001)

- Assessment of applicability to the company
- Preparation of the Organizational, Management, and Control Model (Modello 231)
- Appointment of the Supervisory Body (Organismo di Vigilanza)
- Staff training on 231 compliance protocols

### GDPR Compliance

- Data protection impact assessments (DPIA)
- Drafting of internal data protection policies and procedures
- Records of Processing Activities (RoPA)
- Data Processing Agreements (DPA) with third-party processors
- Appointment of Data Protection Officer (DPO) where required
- Italian Supervisory Authority (Garante Privacy) compliance monitoring

### Labor Law Compliance

- Employment contract drafting in compliance with Italian labor law (Codice del Lavoro)
- Application of correct National Collective Bargaining Agreement (CCNL)
- Mandatory notifications to labor authorities (Centro per l'Impiego)
- Health and safety compliance (D.Lgs. 81/2008)
- Employee dismissal procedures in compliance with Italian law
- Equal opportunity and anti-discrimination compliance

### Sector-Specific Licenses and Authorizations

- SCIA (Segnalazione Certificata di Inizio Attività) for qualifying activities
- Municipal and prefectural permits where required
- Industry-specific licenses (financial services, food & beverage, pharmaceuticals, etc.)

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with Italian obligations can result in:

- Administrative fines from Italian tax authorities (ranging from hundreds to tens of thousands of euros)
- Personal liability for directors in certain circumstances
- Suspension or revocation of business licenses
- Reputational damage with Italian business partners and authorities

ABAG's compliance service is designed to eliminate this risk entirely.
