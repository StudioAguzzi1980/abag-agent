# Service: Market Entry Strategy

## Service Overview

Entering a new market requires more than legal registration and a bank account. It demands a clear understanding of the market dynamics, customer expectations, competitive landscape, and regulatory environment. ABAG's Market Entry Strategy service helps U.S. companies develop a well-grounded plan for entering Italy and the broader European market — and then supports execution.

---

## What Market Entry Strategy Covers

### Phase 1 — Market Assessment

- Analysis of the target industry and sector in Italy
- Competitive landscape mapping
- Identification of target customer segments and distribution channels
- Regulatory environment review specific to the industry
- Assessment of cultural and commercial considerations for U.S. brands in Italy
- Identification of barriers to entry and mitigation strategies

### Phase 2 — Business Model Adaptation

- Review of the U.S. business model and its applicability to Italy/Europe
- Pricing strategy localization
- Product and service adaptation for Italian market requirements (labeling, standards, certification)
- Assessment of localization requirements (language, invoicing, contracts)
- Brand positioning for the Italian market

### Phase 3 — Entry Mode Selection

ABAG advises on the optimal market entry mode:

| Mode | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct subsidiary** | Establish an Italian S.r.l. or S.p.A. | Companies planning significant long-term presence |
| **Branch office** | Extend the U.S. parent's legal presence | Companies testing the market with full liability acceptance |
| **Distribution agreement** | Partner with Italian distributor | Product companies not yet ready for direct presence |
| **Commercial agent** | Appoint an Italian sales agent | Companies wanting market representation without local entity |
| **Joint venture** | Partner with Italian company | Companies seeking local operational expertise |
| **Representative office** | Non-commercial Italian presence | Pre-launch market research and relationship building |

### Phase 4 — Operational Roadmap

- Phased market entry plan with milestones and timelines
- Resource requirements: headcount, office space, capital
- Technology and IT infrastructure for Italian operations
- Supply chain and logistics assessment (where relevant)
- Hiring plan for local staff

### Phase 5 — Incentives and Funding

ABAG identifies available EU and Italian incentives, including:

- **PNRR (Piano Nazionale di Ripresa e Resilienza)** — Italian recovery fund opportunities
- **Simest loans** — Subsidized financing for Italian export companies (and their foreign partners)
- **Invitalia grants** — Italian government grants for qualifying sectors
- **ICE (Italian Trade Agency)** — Support programs for international businesses in Italy
- **EU structural funds** — Available in certain Italian regions (particularly South Italy)
- **R&D tax credits** — Available for qualifying Italian R&D activities
