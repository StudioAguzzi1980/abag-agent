# ABAG Services — Overview

## About American Business Advisory Group

American Business Advisory Group (ABAG) is a professional advisory firm dedicated to supporting U.S. companies and entrepreneurs in establishing and growing their operations in Italy and across Europe. ABAG bridges the gap between American business culture and the Italian regulatory, fiscal, and commercial landscape.

Our team combines international expertise with deep local knowledge, providing a single point of coordination for every aspect of your Italian and European business journey.

## How ABAG Works

ABAG operates as your trusted advisor and coordinator throughout the business lifecycle:

1. **Discovery** — We learn about your business, objectives, and constraints
2. **Strategy** — We recommend the right legal structure, tax approach, and market entry plan
3. **Execution** — We manage the administrative, legal, and regulatory processes on your behalf
4. **Ongoing Support** — We provide continuous compliance, accounting, and advisory services post-launch
