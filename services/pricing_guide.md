## Bundle Packages

ABAG offers bundled packages for clients entering Italy for the first time, combining:

- Company formation
- Registered office (12 months)
- Banking support
- First 3 months of accounting
- Annual compliance monitoring

**Indicative bundle price:** €8,000 – €18,000 depending on entity type and complexity

---

## Payment Terms

- Project fees: 50% at engagement, 50% on completion (or as specified in engagement letter)
- Monthly retainers: billed in advance at the beginning of each month
- Out-of-pocket costs (government fees, notarial charges, travel): charged at cost with supporting receipts

---

## Fee Currency

All ABAG fees are quoted and invoiced in **EUR (Euro)**. U.S. clients may pay via international wire transfer or other agreed payment methods.
