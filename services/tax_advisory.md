# Service: Tax Advisory

## Service Overview

ABAG provides comprehensive Italian and cross-border tax advisory services to U.S.-owned businesses and individuals operating in Italy. Given the intersection of U.S. and Italian tax systems, expert guidance is essential to ensure compliance, minimize double taxation, and structure operations efficiently.

---

## Core Tax Advisory Services

### Italian Corporate Tax Compliance

- Preparation and filing of annual corporate income tax returns (IRES, IRAP)
- Quarterly advance tax payment calculations and filings
- Deferred tax analysis and planning
- Tax risk assessments and regularization

### VAT (IVA) Advisory and Compliance

- VAT registration (Partita IVA) in Italy
- Monthly and quarterly VAT return preparation and filing
- VAT recovery on purchases
- Intra-EU and export/import VAT treatment
- Electronic invoicing (fatturazione elettronica) compliance

### U.S.–Italy Cross-Border Tax Planning

- Application of the U.S.–Italy Double Taxation Treaty (signed 1984, protocol 1999)
- Foreign tax credit planning for U.S. parent companies
- Controlled Foreign Corporation (CFC) analysis under U.S. tax law
- Permanent establishment risk assessment
- Profit repatriation strategies (dividends, royalties, management fees)

### Transfer Pricing

- Documentation of intercompany transactions (transfer pricing file)
- Arm's length analysis and benchmarking
- Preparation of the Italian Master File and Local File
- Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR) support

### Personal Tax Advisory

- Italian personal income tax (IRPEF) for expatriates and relocated executives
- Residency determination and tax domicile planning
- Italian flat-tax regime for new residents (Regime dei Neo-Residenti — €100,000 annual flat tax)
- U.S. expatriate tax obligations (FBAR, FATCA, Form 2555, Form 1116)
- Social security implications for employees working in Italy

### Withholding Taxes

- Dividends, interest, and royalties paid to non-resident entities
- Treaty-reduced withholding tax applications
- Documentation requirements for treaty benefits

|---|---|---|
| IRES (Corporate Income Tax) | 24% | Taxable profit |
| IRAP (Regional Production Tax) | 3.9% (standard) | Adjusted production value |
| IVA (VAT) | 22% standard / 10% / 5% / 4% reduced | Taxable supply value |

## U.S.–Italy Tax Treaty Highlights

The Convention for the Avoidance of Double Taxation between the U.S. and Italy was signed in 1984 and updated by protocol in 1999. Key provisions include:

- **Dividends:** Maximum withholding reduced to 5% (for qualifying corporate shareholders with ≥25% ownership) or 15% (all other cases)
- **Interest:** Maximum withholding of 10%
- **Royalties:** Maximum withholding of 8%
- **Business profits:** Taxable in Italy only if the U.S. company has a permanent establishment (PE) in Italy
- **Capital gains:** Generally exempt from Italian withholding for U.S. residents (with exceptions for real property)

## Tax Planning Opportunities

ABAG helps clients identify and legitimately exploit available tax incentives, including:

- **Patent Box regime** — Preferential tax treatment on income from intellectual property
- **Research & Development (R&D) tax credits** — Available for qualifying Italian R&D activities
- **Industria 4.0 / Transition 5.0** — Investment tax credits for qualifying capital expenditures
- **Super-ammortamento / Iper-ammortamento** — Enhanced depreciation for qualifying assets
- **SEZ (Special Economic Zones) and ZES incentives** — Reduced IRES rates in qualifying southern Italian areas
- **Startup incentives** — Reduced compliance burden and investor deductions for certified innovative startups
